WHAT IS IN THIS BUNDLE
----------------------
1. Keep the Proof - the handbook (PDF)
   The full system: what career evidence is, the five-part workflow,
   the capture and translation tools, six worked examples, the
   confidentiality standard, the 60-minute setup, and the monthly and
   quarterly routines. Read this first.

2. Career Evidence Ledger - the reusable companion (PDF)
   The six fillable forms from the handbook, gathered so you can reuse
   them for years: Quick Capture, Full Entry, Translation worksheet,
   the Monthly Proof Sweep, the Quarterly Proof Review, and the
   Quarterly Evidence Index.

HOW TO USE THE FILLABLE FORMS
-----------------------------
Both PDFs have fillable fields. Open them in any standard PDF reader
(Adobe Acrobat Reader, Preview on macOS, or your browser's PDF viewer)
and type directly into the boxes, then save. You can also print any
page and write by hand. Because your record grows past a single copy,
print or duplicate the forms you use most - the Quick Capture and the
Full Entry.

THE ONE RULE
------------
You record your own recollection of your work and information you are
permitted to keep. You never copy, forward, or reconstruct anything
your employer owns. When permission is unclear, you leave it out.

A NOTE
------
Keep the Proof is educational and is not legal advice. It cannot
interpret your specific employment agreement, your confidentiality
obligations, or your employer's policies. Where a question of
permission genuinely matters, ask your manager, your human resources
team, or an attorney.
