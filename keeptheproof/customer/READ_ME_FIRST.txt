WHAT IS IN THIS BUNDLE
----------------------
1. Keep the Proof - the handbook (PDF, 41 pages)
   The full system: what career evidence is, the five-part workflow,
   the capture and translation tools, six worked examples, the
   confidentiality standard, the 60-minute setup, and the monthly and
   quarterly routines. It ends with a set of reusable fillable pages:
   a one-per-page Quick Capture and a three-page Full Career Evidence
   Entry. Read this first.

2. Career Evidence Ledger - the reusable companion (PDF, 12 pages)
   The six reusable form sets from the handbook, gathered so you can
   reuse them for years: Quick Capture (one per page), the three-page
   Full Career Evidence Entry, the Translation Worksheet, the Proof
   Line Builder, the Monthly Proof Sweep, the Quarterly Proof Review,
   and the Quarterly Evidence Index. Each PDF has real bookmarks, so
   you can jump straight to any form.

BEFORE YOU BEGIN
----------------
Before you begin, save a copy of each PDF under a new name. Keep the
original files as blank masters so you can return to clean forms
whenever you need them.

HOW TO USE THE FILLABLE FORMS
-----------------------------
Both PDFs have fillable form fields, and the longer answer fields are
multiline so a full response stays visible. For the most reliable
filling, saving, and printing, we recommend Adobe Acrobat Reader (free).
Other readers and in-browser PDF viewers can open and fill the forms as
well, though they do not all behave identically, so if a field will not
save in one, open the file in Acrobat Reader. Every page is also built
to print cleanly if you would rather write by hand. Because your record
grows past a single copy, print or duplicate the forms you use most: the
Quick Capture and the Full Career Evidence Entry.

THE ONE RULE
------------
You record your own recollection of your work and information you are
permitted to keep. You never copy, forward, or reconstruct anything
your employer owns. Generalizing information does not create permission.
When permission is unclear, you leave it out.

A NOTE
------
Keep the Proof is educational and is not legal advice. It cannot
interpret your specific employment agreement, your confidentiality
obligations, or your employer's policies. Where a question of
permission genuinely matters, ask your manager, your human resources
team, or an attorney.
