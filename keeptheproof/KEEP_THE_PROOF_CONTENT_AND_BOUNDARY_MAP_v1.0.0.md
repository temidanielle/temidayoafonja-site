## 2. The five-part workflow (recurring visual, no forced acronym)

**Capture · Clarify · Translate · Protect · Retrieve**

| Step | What it means |
|---|---|
| Capture | Record it while the facts are fresh |
| Clarify | Separate the team result from your own part |
| Translate | Put it in words an outsider understands |
| Protect | Keep only what you are permitted to hold |
| Retrieve | Tag it so you can find it when it matters |

The order is taught as load-bearing (Capture before Clarify; Protect before Retrieve). It is presented as a five-cell navy strip and referenced throughout, never compressed into an acronym.

## 3. The tools

- **Two-Minute Quick Capture** — five prompts, for catching work before it fades.
- **Full Career Evidence Entry** — the complete entry for work worth keeping, with fields for formal responsibility vs. actual ownership, judgment, scope, outcome, problem prevented, quantitative and qualitative evidence, confidentiality check, and retrieval tags.
- **Translation system** — internal language → portable language, with an eight-transformation table.
- **The Proof Line** — the portable end product: one accurate, plain-language sentence built from condition, your part, scope, outcome, and support.

## 6. The confidentiality standard (the core of the product)

Stated standard, verbatim: **"Permission comes before protection. A secure personal device does not make information yours to retain."**

Three visual tiers (KEEP / CARE / NEVER):

- **KEEP** (appropriate) — your own high-level recollection, publicly disclosed outcomes, and anything your employer has expressly permitted you to retain.
- **CARE** (needs care) — numbers, client or project detail, internal context that may be sensitive; generalize, seek permission, or default downward.
- **NEVER** — source code, credentials, security settings, customer or employee data, unreleased product detail, internal financials, privileged or legal material, trade secrets, internal decks, and any employer-owned document or file.

Explicit teachings:

- Personal-device use, a personal account, or non-working-hours use does **not** transfer ownership or create permission.
- Never copy, forward, screenshot, download, photograph, print, upload, or reconstruct employer-owned material into any personal system.
- Never circumvent security controls, data-loss tools, access restrictions, monitoring, or retention rules.
- Never paste confidential or employer-owned information into an AI system of any kind.
- When permission is uncertain, omit the information and seek qualified advice.
- The guide is educational and **not legal advice**; ask a manager, HR, or an attorney where permission genuinely matters.

## 7. Where the product deliberately stops (the boundary)

The handbook names this on the "Where Keep the Proof ends" page. It **does not**:

- Score work with any density, optionality, or capacity metric.
- Assess a "career state" or place anyone in a quadrant or set of states.
- Advise whether to stay or leave a role, or produce a move plan.
- Teach resume writing, interview coaching, networking scripts, salary/job-search tactics.

A single quiet closing pointer notes that a separate tool, the Capability Formation Field Kit, is designed for interpreting position — mentioned once, never taught here.
