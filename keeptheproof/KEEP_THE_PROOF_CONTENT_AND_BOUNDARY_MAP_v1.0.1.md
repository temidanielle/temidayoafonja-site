Category the product establishes: **career evidence** - a private, permitted,
retrievable record of your own work.

Central promise: build a private, usable record of your work *before* a review,
promotion, job search, or unexpected change forces you to reconstruct it from
memory.

Opening question (verbatim): *"If your work access disappeared this afternoon,
how much of your career evidence would disappear with it?"*

## 2. The five-part workflow (recurring visual, no forced acronym)

**Capture | Clarify | Translate | Protect | Retrieve**

| Step | What it means |
|---|---|
| Capture | Record it while the facts are fresh |
| Clarify | Separate the team result from your own part |
| Translate | Put it in words an outsider understands |
| Protect | Keep only what you are permitted to hold |
| Retrieve | Tag it so you can find it when it matters |

The order is taught as load-bearing (Capture before Clarify; Protect before
Retrieve). It is presented as a five-cell navy strip and referenced throughout,
never compressed into an acronym.

## 3. The tools

- **Two-Minute Quick Capture** - five prompts, one capture per page, for
  catching work before it fades. Prompts: what happened; specific contribution
  or judgment; what changed, improved, became possible, or was prevented;
  verifier role or permitted public reference; confidential detail to keep out.
- **Full Career Evidence Entry** - a three-page, twenty-field form, each taught
  field on its own line (date or period; project or work event; situation or
  need; why it mattered; formal responsibility; actual ownership; decision or
  judgment exercised; actions taken; people and functions involved; scope and
  constraint; outcome or observable change; problem prevented; quantitative
  evidence, accurate and permitted; qualitative evidence or validation; team
  result vs. your honest part; internal wording, before translation;
  portable-language version; permitted evidence reference; confidentiality and
  permission check; retrieval tags). Identical in wording, order, and logic in
  the handbook and the ledger.
- **Translation system** - internal language to portable language, framed as
  eight translation moves plus one protection rule (the ninth table row is the
  protection rule, not a translation move); the worksheet fields are multiline.
- **The Proof Line** - the portable end product: one accurate, plain-language
  sentence built from condition, your part, scope, outcome, and support. In the
  ledger it is a standalone Proof Line Builder form.

## 5. Routines and use

- The complete **60-minute setup** (a six-row time-boxed table).
- **Monthly Proof Sweep** (10-15 minutes) and **Quarterly Proof Review**
  (about 30 minutes), plus the **Quarterly Evidence Index**.
- **Retrieval** guidance: which entries to pull for each occasion.
- **Starting late**: honest reconstruction when the record does not yet exist.
- An **optional, privacy-conscious AI prompt**, gated by an explicit pre-rule
  that no confidential material ever enters an AI system.

## 6. The confidentiality standard (the core of the product)

Stated standard, verbatim: **"Permission comes before protection. A secure
personal device does not make information yours to retain."**

Three visual tiers (KEEP / CARE / NEVER):

- **KEEP** (appropriate) - your own high-level recollection, publicly disclosed
  outcomes, and anything your employer has expressly permitted you to retain.
- **CARE** (needs care) - numbers, client or project detail, internal context
  that may be sensitive; **seek permission, use only what is already public, or
  leave it out**, and default downward when unsure.
- **NEVER** - source code, credentials, security settings, customer or employee
  data, unreleased product detail, internal financials, privileged or legal
  material, trade secrets, internal decks, and any employer-owned document or
  file.

Explicit teachings:

- Personal-device use, a personal account, or non-working-hours use does **not**
  transfer ownership or create permission.
- **Generalizing information does not create permission**; use generalized
  wording only after confirming you are permitted to retain the underlying
  information.
- Never copy, forward, screenshot, download, photograph, print, upload, or
  reconstruct employer-owned material into any personal system.
- Never circumvent security controls, data-loss tools, access restrictions,
  monitoring, or retention rules.
- Never paste confidential or employer-owned information into an AI system of
  any kind.
- Verifiers are recorded by role or public reference, never a colleague's
  personal details; permitted-evidence references name a source or location and
  never paste the artifact or confidential content.
- When permission is uncertain, omit the information and seek qualified advice.
- The guide is educational and **not legal advice**; ask a manager, HR, or an
  attorney where permission genuinely matters.

## 7. Where the product deliberately stops (the boundary)

The handbook names this on the "Where Keep the Proof ends" page. It **does
not**:

- Score work with any density, optionality, or capacity metric.
- Assess a "career state" or place anyone in a quadrant or set of states.
- Advise whether to stay or leave a role, or produce a move plan.
- Teach resume writing, interview coaching, networking scripts, salary or
  job-search tactics.
- Provide a Career Portability Map or an AI Role Relevance Audit.

A single quiet closing pointer notes that **a separate tool from The Density
Group**, the Capability Formation Field Kit, is designed for interpreting
position - mentioned once, never taught here, and with no price stated.
