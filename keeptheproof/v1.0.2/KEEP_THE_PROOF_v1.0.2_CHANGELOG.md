## 4. "Starting Late" expanded into a full module
Replaced the one-page "If your access has already disappeared" with a five-page module,
**"Starting Late: When Your Access Is Already Gone."**
- Steady opening for readers reading after a reduction or change; no job-market promises.
- **Permission rule kept word for word:** "Your own memory is yours. Publicly disclosed
  outcomes are usable. Anything a former employer expressly permitted you to keep is
  usable. What is not usable is a copy of anything you took, or access you were not
  meant to retain. The rule does not relax because the timing is inconvenient."
- Printable **"Reconstruct by Period"** worksheet: one row per role or year, with
  columns Major projects / Decisions I made / Problems I caught / Feedback I remember.
- **Memory anchors** list: a hard call; a launch, delivery, or deadline; a problem
  caught early; a piece of feedback; a moment someone relied on you.
- **Confidence markers** on every reconstructed entry: Sure / Approximate / Unsure,
  with the rule that Approximate and Unsure entries do not become Proof Lines until
  confirmed.
- A **60-minute "starting late" setup** table mirroring the existing 60-minute setup.
- No new legal or permission claims beyond what the guide already states.
