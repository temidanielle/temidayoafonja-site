# Keep the Proof — Gumroad product description (v1.0.2 draft)

Draft, ready to paste. No em dashes, no hype words. ~145 words.

---

Keep the Proof is a system for building a private record of your work, so you can prove
your value before a review, a promotion case, or an unexpected change.

With about 60 minutes of focused time, in one sitting or several, you will complete your
first Career Evidence Entry.

You will capture what you did, separate your contribution from the team's, translate it
into portable language, and keep only what you are permitted to keep.

Inside: the guided handbook, the reusable Career Evidence Ledger, and a Start Here page.
Every form is printable. Print the pages you need, or copy the prompts into any notebook
you control.

Already lost access to your work systems? A Starting Late module walks you through
rebuilding an honest record from memory.

Applying for a specific role? A Match Your Proof to a Role page lines your evidence up
against one posting.

Educational, not legal advice.
