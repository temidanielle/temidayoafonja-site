The question underneath the good review

You can be performing well, trusted with real responsibility, and succeeding inside your current organization, and still not be able to answer the one question that travels with you: what is this job building in me now, and what could I actually take somewhere else?

The Field Kit reads your career position and puts a number on what standing still costs. Twelve statements, mandatory evidence, built-in calibration, and a dated result you revisit quarterly. Not a quiz. A working instrument.

You score each statement against the last ninety days of your actual working life, support every score with evidence, and read what the pattern says about two things:

  • Density, your learning velocity. The capability your current environment is actually building in you, which has little to do with tenure.
  • Optionality, your market portability. Whether that capability is yours to carry, and whether the people who could use it have seen it.

Together, those two scores tell you:

  • Your state, or your boundary. Where the two scores place you, or which two states you sit between.
  • The evidence underneath the score. The specific ninety-day instances that hold each number up.
  • Where your position may be exposed. The quiet risk a strong review can hide.

It is a structured working instrument. It is not career coaching, a personality test, or a prediction of your future.
