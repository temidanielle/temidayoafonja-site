Company overview

SPY Group is a service-disabled veteran-owned small business providing federal procurement support, supply chain management, and logistics execution. The company is founded and led by a 100 percent disabled veteran, and carries small business, veteran, and minority-owned designations.

We work two ways: directly with contracting offices on micro-purchase, simplified-acquisition, and SDVOSB set-aside requirements, and as a subcontractor to prime contractors carrying small-business subcontracting plans.

Commodity fulfillment against defined product lines: MRO and facility consumables, safety equipment and PPE, IT peripherals, janitorial and sanitation supply, office products. Categories under evaluation and selected for lead-time reliability and compliant sourcing.

Differentiators

  • Award authority, not just a label. SDVOSB status supports set-aside and sole-source awards and credits against agency small-business goaling, shortening the procurement timeline.
  • Documentation as it happens. Custody, receipt, and price reasonableness are recorded during execution, so the contract file is defensible on the day it is reviewed.
  • Sized for the requirement. A purchase-card order receives the same attention as a task order, and the point of contact is the person accountable for the delivery date.
  • Stacked designations. Veteran, service-disabled, minority-owned, and small — one supplier satisfying several participation categories at once.
