Paths to award

Four ways to put work here, by ceiling.

SDVOSB status is not a courtesy line on a website — it is an acquisition authority. These are the mechanisms a buyer can actually use, from a card purchase up to a sole-source award, ordered by dollar ceiling.

$10KCeiling

Government purchase card

Micro-purchase. A card holder buys directly, no competition required, and the spend counts toward the agency's small-business and veteran goals.

$250KCeiling

Simplified acquisition

Streamlined procedures below the simplified acquisition threshold. Acquisitions in this range are reserved for small business — quotes turned in a week, not a quarter.

$4.5MServices & supplies

SDVOSB set-aside or sole source

A contracting officer may set an acquisition aside for service-disabled veteran-owned small businesses, or award sole-source without competition when the criteria are met. Manufacturing carries a higher ceiling.

No capSubcontract

Teaming & subcontracting

For primes carrying a small-business subcontracting plan: SDVOSB subcontract dollars count against the plan's goals. Joint ventures and mentor-protégé arrangements welcome.

Thresholds shown reflect current FAR small-business rules and are reviewed periodically. Confirm the figure that applies to your specific acquisition before relying on it.

Differentiators

Why the file closes cleanly.

Three reasons a buyer or a prime ends up better off, written the way they would have to justify the choice in writing.

Award authority, not just a label

Service-disabled veteran-owned status opens set-aside and sole-source paths that shorten a procurement timeline and credit against agency goaling. The socio-economic categories stack: veteran, minority-owned, small.

Documentation as a habit

Founded and led by a veteran. Custody, receipt, and price reasonableness are documented as the work happens, so the contract file is defensible on the day it is audited rather than reconstructed later.

Sized for the work

Small enough that a purchase-card order gets the same attention as a task order, and direct enough that the person who answers the phone is the person accountable for the delivery date.
