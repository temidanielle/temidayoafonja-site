## 1. Executive recommendation

Keep the Proof does not need a rebuild. It needs a re-sequencing and two genuine additions, inside the same intellectual property, the same name, and the same price.

The single most consistent finding across the brief and both research passes is a mismatch of location, not of quality. The product's method solves the two problems a person can finish alone: I cannot remember what I did, and I cannot explain what I did. Its marketing and its opening speak mostly to the two problems that need a destination and other people: what of this is relevant elsewhere, and how do I get recognized. Version 2 should stop trying to close that second gap inside the record and instead do the first job so well, and enter it so many ways, that the buyer recognizes themselves on the first page and is doing real work by the third.

Five moves define Version 2:

1. Re-spine the product on CAPTURE, CLARIFY, CARRY, folding the current five-step method into three stages a reader can hold in their head, with CLARIFY as the intellectual center.
2. Open with recognition, not education. A short "why you might be here" page turns the six research situations into a mirror, under one working logic: different circumstances, same first job, which is to build an accurate account of the work.
3. Build a real second door. A substantially stronger RECONSTRUCT path, entered with "Already lost access? Start here," for the person whose systems already closed. This is the largest documented product gap.
4. Add the two things the evidence supports and competitors lack: a narrow corroboration step, who could confirm this and what they observed, and lawful reconstruction channels, both subject to legal review.
5. Change the format, not the content, of the working record. Keep the beautiful guided handbook and offer the fill-in tools as a copyable, personally controlled document rather than only a fillable PDF that is hard to search and maintain over years.

Two things the brief settles that Version 2 will honor without further debate: remove "60-Minute" from the primary descriptor while preserving "Your First 60 Minutes" as an internal first-session experience, and keep destination-specific translation, direction, and stay-or-leave questions out of Keep the Proof, at the seam with the Field Kit.

Everything about cadence, the exact placement and volume of permission content, price, a visibility bridge, and whether the paid center of gravity should tilt toward reconstruction remains a test, not a decision. Those are named in Sections 21 to 23 and must not be silently implemented.

Recommended proposed page count range: 44 to 56 pages for the guided handbook, plus a separate copyable Professional Record. The reasoning is in Section 24.

## 2. Current product diagnosis: what is already strong and must survive

The current handbook is a genuinely good product. Research Pass 2, run adversarially against more than thirty substitutes, could not find another product that combines what this one does. The following are load-bearing and must survive Version 2 intact.

- The permission standard and the Keep, Care, Never tiers (handbook pages 11 to 14). Unique across every substitute reviewed, aligned with employment counsel and with how insider-risk tools now monitor departing employees. Research labels it KEEP at high confidence and the strongest differentiator found. It errs toward restraint, so it is safe in direction.
- The honesty engine: team result versus your own part (page 22), never invent a number (page 20), and the capture of judgment and prevention (pages 19 to 21). Peer-reviewed evidence on prevention invisibility and on AI fabrication both support this. Labeled KEEP at high confidence.
- The evidence-versus-artifact boundary (page 9) and the filter for what is worth capturing (page 8). This is the discipline that makes the record safe and small. It must not be diluted.
- The non-technical worked examples (Devin, operations; Maya, people and HR; Theo, security; Grace, engineering management; Priya, audit; Sam, clinical research) and the "edge of the tool" boundary page (page 40). Every purpose-built competitor targets technology; these examples are a differentiator, not decoration.
- The Proof Line as the portable end product (page 24), the translation moves (page 23), and the before-and-after demonstration (page 25). Lean, honest, and the clearest teaching in the book.
- The quiet, honest voice throughout, and the operating summary (page 39). The book never overpromises in the body. The tone is right for experienced professionals and must be protected.

What is structurally underpowered today, and why Version 2 exists:

- Reconstruction is about a page and a half (pages 32 to 33) for what the research shows is the job with the strongest evidence of need and of existing spend.
- Corroboration exists only as a single "verifier role" field, when the research shows senior hiring turns on other people almost entirely.
- The opening educates before it recognizes. The buyer meets a definition before they meet themselves.
- The fillable PDF is a poor long-term record format: hard to search, one entry per page, weak on phones, and it duplicates the Ledger.
- The word "proof" quietly promises the tier above what most entries deliver, which are honest examples, not documentary evidence.

## 3. Proposed Version 2 promise and scope

Name (unchanged, per brief): KEEP THE PROOF

Primary descriptor (per brief): A guided system for building your professional record

Supporting line (per brief): For performance reviews, internal moves, career pivots, re-entry and unexpected change.

Price (unchanged in Stage 1, per brief): $49

Removed from the primary descriptor (per brief): "60-Minute." Preserved as an internal experience mechanic only: "Your First 60 Minutes," a focused first session, never a promise that a career can be reconstructed in an hour.

The core job, stated for Version 2. Keep the Proof helps an experienced professional build a private, accurate, personally controlled account of their work: what happened, what they contributed, what judgment they exercised, what decisions they made, what changed, what they prevented, what scope they carried, what part of a team result was actually theirs, what they can responsibly retain and say, and who could corroborate it. It is built for work that leaves a weak automatic trail, and it is especially able to represent senior non-technical contribution: judgment, prioritization, risk reduction, prevention, stakeholder alignment, people development, influence, cross-functional coordination, escalation, ambiguity handling, relationship repair, organizational memory, knowing what not to do, and scope absorbed without a title change.

In scope: recall, attribution, accurate capture, generic translation, permission-aware recordkeeping, preparation, and corroboration readiness.

Explicitly out of scope, preserved as honesty (per brief and research): hiring-market conditions, age bias, credential and licensing requirements, promotion committees, sponsorship, talent hoarding, organizational politics, compensation, employer recognition, level preservation, and whether another context values the capability. The product names these plainly and never claims to move them.

The boundary that protects the ladder. Keep the Proof performs generic translation only. It does not decide whether experience is relevant to a specific target role, organization, or industry, and it does not decide what must be relearned. Destination-specific translation, what travels, and what must be relearned live at the seam with the Field Kit, the Career Move Review, and the wider Capability Formation work. Keep the Proof stops at the edge and points, it does not cross.

## 4. Proposed reader journey, from opening to completion

The journey is designed so the buyer feels recognized before they are educated, and is doing something real within the first few pages.

1. Recognize yourself. A one-page mirror: several plain situations (a review is coming, I was promoted in responsibility only, my access just closed, I have been here fifteen years, I am going back after a break, we were acquired). The reader finds their own sentence. Logic on the page: different circumstances, same first job, build an accurate account of the work.
2. Choose your door. Two entries, named honestly. "Capturing now? Start here." and "Already lost access? Start here." The second routes into RECONSTRUCT without the reader feeling they failed.
3. Understand what you are keeping, briefly. A short Part One: what a professional record is and is not, why it goes missing, the filter, the evidence-versus-artifact line, and the claim, example, evidence, corroboration hierarchy. Shorter than today, because recognition already did the motivating.
4. Do the first session. Your First 60 Minutes: a guided setup that ends with real material captured, one contribution clarified, one Proof Line written, one corroborator named, one private home chosen, and one trigger set for next time. A parallel first session exists for the reconstructor.
5. Learn the method by using it: CAPTURE, then CLARIFY, then CARRY, each taught through completed examples rather than empty boxes, permission woven through rather than front-loaded (final placement is a test, Section 21).
6. Build the reusable record. Move the working tools into a copyable Professional Record the reader owns, with the fillable PDF retained as an option.
7. Keep it current. Event-based capture as the spine, with the existing monthly and quarterly routines preserved as an option, because the cadence question is not settled.
8. Reach the edge, and stop. The boundary page and the quiet Field Kit pointer. The record's job is done when it is honest, portable, and safe to keep.

## 8. CAPTURE architecture

Question the reader is answering: what actually happened?

- Primary tool, unchanged: the Two-Minute Quick Capture (five prompts). It remains the fast, low-friction entry point.
- New: event-based capture. A trigger list (the eleven events from the brief) gives the reader a reason to open the record, matching the research finding that every documented capture trigger is an event, not a date. This is memory-retrieval support: the reader scans the triggers and recognizes what happened, rather than facing a blank page.
- Retained: the Full Professional Record Entry for work worth keeping in depth.
- Coexistence, not replacement. Event-based capture is presented as the spine, and the existing monthly sweep and quarterly review are preserved as an optional rhythm. The research labels cadence TEST BEFORE CHANGING, with two working models both reported as viable (light ongoing capture, or a reconstruction session every six to twelve months). Version 2 shows how event capture and the routines coexist and lets the buyer's own behavior settle the question later.
- Memory support built in. The reconstruct route and the event grid both exist to lower the cost of remembering, which the research names as the core solved problem.

What CAPTURE does not do: it does not judge relevance to a destination, and it does not decide what to keep beyond the permission filter.

## 9. CLARIFY architecture

Question the reader is answering: what was actually yours?

This is the intellectual center of Version 2, and where the product is most differentiated. Nothing here is invented; it is the existing intellectual property, gathered and strengthened.

- Assignment versus contribution (from current page 19). The gap between what you were assigned and what you drove is where the real contribution lives.
- Team result versus your part (current page 22). Two sentences, always in order: the shared result, then your specific part. Load-bearing.
- Judgment and decisions (current page 19). Capture the call and the options set aside, the part with no artifact.
- Consequences and no invented numbers (current page 20). Describe the before and after; never manufacture a figure.
- Prevention and invisible work (current page 21). The problem that never happened, captured honestly with the risk, the catch, and the likely path, held to "would likely have."
- Scope (current page 19, and a NEW dedicated page). Scope makes size legible; the new page names the scope-expanded-manager case explicitly.
- Honest attribution throughout. The record neither inflates nor disappears.
- NEW, narrow: corroboration. Who could confirm this, and what did they directly observe. This is corroboration readiness, captured while memory is fresh. It is explicitly not networking instruction and not a relationship course. The boundary is stated on the page.

Why CLARIFY carries the weight. The research shows that AI has made wording cheap and that the durable, scarce value is the honest raw account of judgment, prevention, and attribution, which is exactly what CLARIFY produces. Keeping this section strong and the CARRY (translation) section lean is the deliberate posture.

## 10. CARRY architecture

Question the reader is answering: what can you responsibly take forward?

- Translate (current page 23). Generic translation only: internal language into portable language, the eight moves plus the protection rule. Kept lean on purpose.
- Retain context and scope. Portability without context overstates the work; the translation keeps scope and constraint attached.
- The Proof Line (current page 24). The portable end product, up to five ingredients, never an invented number, never sole credit for shared work.
- Permission and confidentiality boundaries travel with everything (the thread from Part Three).
- TEST: the visibility outputs. The honest three forms in which a private record reaches other people (searchable profile and skills keywords, a short forwardable proof line, spoken interview stories). Included only if approved, because the research leaves the visibility bridge unresolved.
- The relearning handoff (NEW seam, brief-approved). CARRY performs generic translation and then stops. It does not decide whether the experience is relevant to a specific destination, and it does not decide what must be relearned. A single prompt names the question and hands off to the Field Kit and the wider Capability Formation work. This is the hard boundary the brief draws, and it is honored precisely.

What CARRY must never become: destination-specific translation, a decision about what travels, a stay-or-leave or direction reading, or a claim that better wording changes labor-market outcomes.

## 11. RECONSTRUCT architecture

Entry language (per brief): Already lost access? Start here.

Principle (per brief): if the change has already happened, the reader can still build a useful professional account. They simply start differently.

This is the largest documented product gap and the job with the strongest evidence of need and of existing paid demand. Version 2 turns a page and a half into a real path.

- A dedicated first-session route so the reconstructor never enters through a capture flow that assumes live access.
- Sources (per brief, subject to legal review): previous resumes, LinkedIn history, documents already lawfully held, personal calendars where appropriate, old job descriptions, certifications, public announcements, press releases, annual reports, public work and artifacts, and former colleagues who directly observed the work.
- Method: reconstruct by period, anchor to clearly remembered moments, write quick captures from memory, expand the strongest, then start capturing in real time so reconstruction is never needed again.
- Corroboration is especially valuable here, because institutional memory disappears at exit and colleagues are often the only remaining witnesses to the work.
- The honesty ceiling, strengthened: you will not recover everything, mark uncertain as uncertain, and an honest approximate record beats a confident wrong one.

CRITICAL LEGAL BOUNDARY. The reconstruction sources and any new permission language are not finalized in this architecture. Everything touching lawful channels, documents held, written permission, and public artifacts is flagged LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED (Sections 15 and 22). Version 2 must not invent legal guidance.

Open strategic question (do not decide in Stage 1): whether the expanded reconstruction lives inside Keep the Proof or becomes a separate paid module. The research and the brief both leave this open. The architecture is built so reconstruction can be a strong part of the handbook and, if later evidence supports it, be lifted into a standalone module without redesign.

## 12. Your First 60 Minutes experience

A focused first session, never a promise that a career can be rebuilt in an hour.

Design hypothesis for the finish line (from the brief, not immutable copy). By the end of the session the reader has:
- 3 pieces of work captured
- 1 contribution clarified in depth
- 1 judgment, decision, or prevention example identified
- 1 portable Proof Line written
- 1 possible corroborator identified
- 1 personally controlled record location established
- 1 clear trigger set for the next capture

Experience principles applied (from the Jessie reference, at the level of experience only, with no imitation of its branding, topic, tone, or aesthetic): visible progress, more doing earlier, shorter instructional chunks, completed examples beside every tool, and one clear next action at each step. The session is a checklist with a visible finish line, not a lecture.

The reconstruct route. For a reader whose access is already gone, the first session runs through RECONSTRUCT instead: gather lawful sources, anchor to remembered moments, produce the first reconstructed captures and one Proof Line, name one corroborator, and set the going-forward trigger. Same finish line, different starting material, no sense of having failed.

Naming note. "Your First 60 Minutes" is an internal experience mechanic. The product does not promise completion in sixty minutes anywhere in the primary descriptor or the marketing.

## 13. Proposed reusable Professional Record structure

The brief asks for a platform-neutral architecture, with no platform chosen without evidence, and Research Pass 2's format section reaches the same conclusion: the fillable PDF feels substantial but is hard to search, hard to reuse, weak on phones, and duplicated across two files.

Two artifacts, clearly separated:

1. The guided handbook. Teaching, examples, decisions, the boundary, and Your First 60 Minutes. This is the beautiful thing the reader reads.
2. The reusable Professional Record. A copyable, personally controlled working document the reader duplicates and maintains over years.

Platform-neutral means the Record is specified as a structure, not a product. It is delivered as a plain, widely readable document the reader can keep in a personal document tool of their choosing, so it stays searchable, duplicable, and owned. The fillable and printable PDF pages are retained as an option for readers who prefer paper or a typed form. No platform (Notion, a spreadsheet, an app) is named or required.

Proposed structure of the Record (fields drawn from the existing tools, evaluated for long-term use):
- A running capture log: one line per event, using the five Quick Capture prompts, easy to add to from a phone.
- Full entries: expanded from the existing field set. The current twenty fields are strong but long; propose grouping them into five clusters for maintainability: when and what; formal versus actual ownership; the judgment and the decision; the outcome, the prevention, and the evidence; and translation, corroboration, and retrieval tags. The content is unchanged; the grouping makes a full entry faster to complete and easier to reread.
- A corroboration list: per entry, who could confirm this and what they observed (role and observation only, no stored personal detail).
- Proof Lines: the portable sentences, kept together for reuse.
- An evidence index: a running list so any entry is a search away.

What the Record deliberately omits: any field that invites employer-owned artifacts, any destination-specific targeting, and any direction or stay-or-leave content.

Decision required before Stage 2 (Section 23): whether the reusable Professional Record replaces the standalone Career Evidence Ledger, absorbs it, or ships alongside it as the print companion. The recommendation is that the copyable Record becomes primary and the Ledger's forms are retained as the printable option, removing the duplication.

## 14. Corroboration treatment

The research is specific: employers confirm only title and dates, institutional memory of contribution disappears at exit, and senior hiring turns on backchannel calls to individuals. Corroboration lives with people, not institutions. A private record cannot create access, but it can prepare the people who do.

The defensible, narrow role inside Keep the Proof:
- Per entry, name who could confirm this, by role, and what they directly observed.
- Capture it while memory is fresh, because the witness will not remember forever either.
- Keep it to role and observation. Do not store personal contact details or private information about the colleague inside the record.
- State the boundary on the page: this is corroboration readiness, not networking, not outreach scripting, not a relationship course.

What it is not, and must not drift into: instruction on how to build a network, ask for referrals, or manage relationships. Those are outside the product. The corroboration step stops at readiness.

Legal note: capturing observations attributed to a named person touches privacy expectations. Keep the field to role plus observation, and include this in the legal review (Section 22).

## 15. Permission and legal-review map

The permission standard (Keep, Care, Never, the central rule, lines that do not move) is preserved in full. The research names it the strongest differentiator found and marks it KEEP at high confidence. It is not shortened merely to make the product smaller.

What stays as content (no legal question, keep verbatim): the central rule, the three tiers, the lines that do not move, the evidence-versus-artifact boundary, and the AI confidentiality guardrail.

What is new and requires legal review before any wording is finalized (LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED):
- State personnel-record inspection and copy rights, which vary by state and do not exist federally.
- Documents the employee already lawfully holds (offer letters, signed reviews, pay records, certificates).
- How to secure written permission, given that verbal permission is documented as failing.
- Anonymized process accounts, where the current standard may be stricter than common practice and sits in tension with the product's own translation moves.

- Severance and non-disparagement clauses; preservation exceptions where discrimination or whistleblower claims exist.
- Employer AI-tool policies.
- The corroboration field's handling of another person's observations (privacy).

What is a sequencing test, not a legal question (Section 21): where the permission content sits and how much of it appears before the reader reaches the first tool. The research notes permission currently occupies a large share of the pages and that its friction effect is unmeasured. Version 2 proposes weaving permission through rather than front-loading all of it, but the exact sequence is a test, not a decision.

The posture: keep the standard, complete it only where counsel confirms, and never expand an unreviewed legal claim.

## Appendix: the architecture tested against the quality test

Does the buyer feel recognized before being educated? Yes. The recognition page precedes the definitions.

Does the product help them do something within the first few pages? Yes. Event capture and the first session put the reader into real work early.

Does Capture, Clarify, Carry provide an obvious spine? Yes, three stages replacing five steps.

Can someone who already lost access enter without feeling they failed? Yes. The two-doors router and the reconstruct first session are built for exactly that.

Does the product capture senior work that leaves no obvious artifact? Yes. Prevention, judgment, scope-without-title, coordination, and people development are all covered, several with new examples.

Does it distinguish team result from individual contribution? Yes, retained and made the heart of Clarify.

Does it capture judgment and prevention? Yes, retained and strengthened.

Does it preserve honest permission boundaries? Yes, kept in full and only completed where counsel confirms.

Does it distinguish claim, example, evidence, and corroboration? Yes, a new page does this and answers the "proof" overreach honestly.

Does it avoid implying better wording controls the labor market? Yes. Structural barriers are named as out of scope and the translation content is kept lean.

Does it remain useful to someone who never leaves their employer? Yes. The internal case-builder is served by capture, clarify, and the retrieval and promotion uses.

Does it remain useful to someone returning after time away? Yes, through the reconstruct route and a proposed returner example.

Does it remain useful to someone working above their title? Yes, through the new scope-without-title page and example.

Does it stop before destination-specific career decision work? Yes. The Field Kit seam and the edge-of-the-tool page hold the boundary.

Does the free Starter remain genuinely useful? Yes, unchanged, and the paid job is enlarged instead.

Does the paid product now finish a substantially larger job? Yes: a maintained, permitted, corroboration-ready record with a real reconstruction path, versus one finished accomplishment.

Could a free brag-document template substitute for the whole experience? No. No free template found combines permission, senior non-technical judgment and prevention capture, corroboration readiness, and permission-safe reconstruction after access loss.

Could generic AI create the underlying facts if the user had never recorded them? No, and the architecture is built on that asymmetry: AI polishes wording, it cannot supply judgment, decisions, and prevention that were never recorded. That is why Clarify is the center and translation is kept lean.
