## 1. Correction log

Correction 1. Overbroad talent-decision claim, removed.
- Welcome, middle paragraphs: rewritten. The claim that Temidayo spent eighteen years "close to where talent decisions get made," and that people fared well because they could describe their work, is gone. The new version establishes the warrant ("worked in and around talent, workforce, and organizational decisions") and states plainly that articulation does not decide who is promoted, hired, or selected; sponsorship, opportunity, timing, and conditions do. What a record affects is narrower: how clearly the work can be understood when a decision depends on it.
- About the author: the parallel line ("For eighteen years... close to where talent decisions get made") was softened to "Over nearly two decades... worked in and around workforce strategy, organizational design, and talent decisions," so no claim is made that every year was spent close to talent decisions.

Correction 2. Incomplete first-session sentence, fixed.
- Your First 60 Minutes (capture route): "Before you name one person..." became "Before you finish, name one person who could confirm the work, by role, and note what they directly observed." Corroboration stays narrow.

Correction 3. Cadence, returned to test-before-changing.
- Part Eight, "Keeping it current": rewritten. The claim that tying capture to events is "the lightest way" and "matches how work actually arrives" is gone. The section now presents two useful approaches, event-based capture and a periodic sweep, states that neither is universally better, and tells the reader to use whichever they will sustain, or both. Event triggers remain prominent; the monthly and quarterly routines remain available.
- Operating summary: the rhythm line was reworded to present both cadences without privileging either.
- First-session trigger bullet: "an event rather than only a date" became "an upcoming event or a recurring date both work."
- Professional Record, Section 6, and the review pack's change log and discipline note were updated to match.

Correction 4. AI prompt, narrowed to the approved boundary.
- Part Eight, the optional AI prompt: the requests for "a short resume line, a spoken interview version, and a one-line summary" were removed. The prompt now helps only with removing jargon, producing plain portable language, preserving scope, separating team result from individual contribution, refining a single Proof Line, and flagging vague, inflated, or unsupported wording. A new line forbids it from writing a resume, an interview answer, a profile, or anything tailored to a specific job or employer. The confidentiality guardrail and the "never invent facts, numbers, or results" instruction are unchanged.

Correction 5. Employer-verification claim, bounded.
- Part Four, "Who could confirm this": "Employers will usually confirm only your title and your dates" became "Formal employment verification is often limited to basic details such as your title and your dates, while the people who worked closely with you may remember far more of the contribution itself." The bounded language on what corroboration can and cannot do (strengthen credibility, not decide an employer's choice) is retained.

Correction 6. Legal language, kept legal-gated.
- Part One, "The boundary": a caveat was added stating that the account-versus-artifact distinction is a practical habit, not a legal ruling, and that ownership can be genuinely unsettled in some situations (contract work, work made for hire, signed terms), which are legal questions where the one rule governs. A new LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED note was added to that page.
- "Documents you already lawfully hold" was changed to "Documents you already hold" on both the lawful-channels page and the reconstruction sources page, so the phrasing does not state a legal conclusion; the LEGAL REVIEW notes continue to govern.
- A manuscript-wide scan for definitive legal-sounding statements outside gated blocks was run. Findings and their handling are in the legal list (Section 2). No LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED placeholder was removed or finalized.

Correction 7. "Proof" not overstated.
- The claim-example-evidence-corroboration hierarchy is unchanged and remains the reference. Surrounding copy was adjusted so it does not treat every account as documentary proof: "complete evidence of judgment" became "a complete account of the judgment," and "vague improvement is not evidence. Specific improvement is." became "vague improvement does not persuade. Specific improvement does." The product name is unchanged. This was a copy-discipline pass, not a rename.

Correction 8. Recognition-led editorial pass.
- The manuscript was read for the recognition-before-education standard. It already opens with recognition and keeps explanations short after a distinction is made, and the completed examples carry much of the teaching. The Welcome and cadence rewrites also tightened prose. Findings: the manuscript does not read as a textbook; the few repeated statements of what the system does sit at deliberate summary points (the Carry seam, the edge-of-the-tool page, and the operating summary) and were kept on purpose. No depth was cut to make the product feel thinner.

Correction 9. Whole-read experience test. Results in Section 4.

Correction 10. Professional Record consistency. Audit in Section 3. One change made: the Professional Record's maintenance section was reworded to match the corrected, neutral cadence framing. All fields otherwise match the corrected manuscript.
