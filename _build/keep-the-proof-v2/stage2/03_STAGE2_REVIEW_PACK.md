## 1. Legal-review register

Every point in the manuscript where legal wording is deliberately withheld pending counsel. Each entry gives the location, the purpose, the exact question counsel must answer, and confirms that no interim guidance was invented.

Item L1. The not-legal-advice statement and confidentiality note.
- Location: Front matter, "The record and the fine print."
- Purpose: establish that the guide is educational, not legal advice, and cannot interpret a reader's agreements.
- Exact question for counsel: is the Version 1 disclaimer wording still correct and sufficient for Version 2 as written?
- Wording status: carried from Version 1, held for confirmation. No new claim added.

Item L2. Lawful ways to rebuild the record (the whole page).
- Location: Part Three, "Lawful ways to rebuild the record."
- Purpose: name lawful channels the standard currently omits, so a reader rebuilding after a change knows what they may legitimately use.
- Exact questions for counsel: (a) State-by-state personnel-record inspection and copy rights, including which states, what records, timing, limits, and the absence of a federal right. (b) Which documents an employee may treat as their own lawful copies (offer letters, signed reviews, pay records, certificates, training completions), and any conditions. (c) How to seek and record written permission, and how to describe the documented failure of verbal permission without overreaching. (d) The boundaries of using publicly available work.
- Wording status: page written as named channels and intent only. All specific rights, conditions, and cautions withheld and marked in place. No interim legal guidance provided.

Item L3. Anonymized process accounts.
- Location: cross-cutting, touches Part Three (permission), Part Five (translation), and Part Seven (reconstruction).
- Purpose: reconcile the current strict standard (anonymizing does not create permission) with common professional practice on process-level accounts.
- Exact question for counsel: where, if anywhere, should the standard soften to allow anonymized process accounts, and where must it stay strict? The manuscript currently keeps the strict standard unchanged pending this answer.
- Wording status: no change made; flagged for review.

Item L4. Corroboration and privacy.
- Location: Part Four, "Who could confirm this," and Professional Record, Section 3.
- Purpose: let a reader record who could confirm work and what they observed, without a privacy problem.
- Exact question for counsel: are there privacy considerations in recording a named individual's observations in a private record, and does limiting capture to role and observation, with no stored personal contact detail, resolve them?
- Wording status: manuscript stores role and observation only, no personal contact detail; flagged for confirmation.

Item L5. Reconstruction sources near employer-owned information.
- Location: Part Seven, "Where the record still lives."
- Purpose: list lawful reconstruction sources while keeping the reader clear of anything employer-owned.
- Exact question for counsel: which listed sources (personal calendars, old job descriptions, public announcements, artifacts) carry any retention or ownership risk, and how should each be bounded?
- Wording status: sources listed with a governing note that anything not clearly the reader's is left out; specific boundaries withheld and cross-referenced to L2.

Item L6. The account-versus-artifact ownership caveat.
- Location: Part One, "The boundary," new caveat paragraph and legal note.
- Purpose: keep the core practical distinction between a personal account and an employer-owned artifact, while acknowledging that ownership can be a legal question in some situations.
- Exact question for counsel: how should the guide describe that boundary, and situation-specific ownership (work made for hire, contractor versus employee, signed agreements), without stating a legal conclusion?
- Wording status: the page teaches the practical habit only, a marked legal note is added, and no legal conclusion is stated. Added in the Stage 2 correction pass.

Items also carried for review, not yet written into pages because they do not change reader instructions in Version 2: work-for-hire and portfolio use; contractor versus employee ownership; client confidentiality layered on employer confidentiality; severance and non-disparagement clauses; preservation exceptions; and employer AI-tool and monitoring policies. These inform the permission standard, which is unchanged, and will be revisited with counsel if any reader-facing wording is proposed.

Nothing in the manuscript states a legal conclusion. Every legal item is either carried unchanged from Version 1 or written as a marked placeholder.

## 4. Free-to-paid boundary check

The corrected distinction, per the Stage 1 decision. The paid product is not described as "finishing" a maintained record, because a maintained record is ongoing and cannot truthfully be called finished.

- The Career Evidence Starter helps the reader finish one real piece of work and produce one usable result: one completed entry and one portable Proof Line, in one focused sitting. It remains complete and genuinely useful on its own, and it is not weakened to create differentiation.
- Keep the Proof helps the reader establish the broader professional record they can continue building and return to across performance reviews, internal moves, career pivots, re-entry, and unexpected change.

Why the paid job is materially larger. It establishes the record and the system rather than finishing a single entry; it supports reconstruction after access is gone; it captures senior and invisible work, including scope carried without a title; it separates team result from individual contribution; it captures judgment, decisions, and prevention; it preserves the full permission discipline; it adds narrow corroboration readiness; and it provides a maintainable, personally controlled working home in the Professional Record.

How the two stay distinct in practice. The Starter is one entry, done in an evening, proving the method on a single accomplishment. Keep the Proof is the standing record and the discipline around it, entered from either door, kept over years. A reader who has done the Starter has finished one thing; a reader using Keep the Proof has established something they keep returning to. Nothing in Version 2 shrinks the Starter to make this gap; the gap is the record, the reconstruction, the senior-work capture, the corroboration, and the maintainable home.

The live Starter page currently describes the paid product with superseded language ("the paid 60-minute system," "the paid Career Evidence System"). That copy is logged for a later, separate change to the approved Version 2 descriptor, and the live page is not modified now.

## 5. Product-seam check

Where Keep the Proof stops and the rest of Capability Formation begins. The seam is explicit and honored throughout the manuscript.

Keep the Proof does (inside scope): recall, attribution, accurate capture, general translation into portable language, permission-aware recordkeeping, reconstruction after access loss, preparation for the moments that ask for an account, and corroboration readiness.

Keep the Proof does not do (across the seam): decide whether a specific move is right; determine what definitively travels to a specific destination; decide what an employer will value; advise whether to stay or leave; predict or control hiring, promotion, level, compensation, sponsorship, credentials, licensing, age bias, discrimination, organizational politics, or market conditions.

Where those questions go. Destination-specific translation, what travels, what must be relearned, and whether to move belong to the Field Kit, the Career Move Review, and the wider Capability Formation work. Organizational and enterprise capability questions belong to the organizational side of Capability Formation, not to this product.

How the seam appears in the manuscript, in three places, without becoming defensive or repetitive:
- Part Five, "Where the record becomes useful," names the moments and explicitly stops before teaching interview, resume, or move mechanics.
- Part Five, "What would not travel," names the destination question, hands it off, and does not answer it.
- Closing, "The edge of the tool," states the boundary once, plainly, and points to the Field Kit.

The manuscript states the structural limits honestly but lightly: it says once, clearly, that a record can be honest and portable and still meet a market, a committee, or a bias it cannot control, and it does not repeat the point defensively.

## 6. Stage 2 quality-test results

The manuscript tested against each question in the approval brief.

- Does the buyer recognize why they may be here before the framework? Yes. "Why you might be here" precedes any definition or method.
- Can they begin doing something useful within the first few pages? Yes. The two doors and Your First 60 Minutes put them into real capture early; event triggers lower the cost of starting.
- Is Capture, Clarify, Carry unmistakable? Yes. It is the spine page, the part structure, and the operating summary.
- Is Clarify genuinely the intellectual center? Yes. It is the largest teaching part and holds team-versus-part, judgment, prevention, scope-without-title, and corroboration.
- Can someone who already lost access enter without feeling they failed? Yes. The second door, the reconstruct first session, and Part Seven's tone are built for exactly that.
- Does it represent senior non-technical work well? Yes. Scope without title, judgment, prevention, people development, coordination, and relationship repair are all captured, in ordinary language before any label.
- Does it capture judgment? Yes, throughout Clarify and in Andre's example.
- Does it capture prevention? Yes, retained and strengthened, with Theo.
- Does it capture scope without title? Yes, a dedicated page and a worked example.
- Does it distinguish team result from individual contribution? Yes, the heart of Clarify, with Grace.
- Does it distinguish claim, example, evidence, and corroboration? Yes, a dedicated page, and it corrects the "proof" overreach honestly.
- Does it preserve permission discipline? Yes, the central rule, Keep/Care/Never, and lines that do not move are unchanged and load-bearing.
- Are legal questions clearly withheld rather than guessed? Yes, marked in place and gathered in the register.
- Does the Professional Record feel maintainable over years? Yes: platform-neutral, searchable, copyable, personally owned, with a living index and light optional routines.
- Useful to someone who never leaves their employer? Yes: reviews, promotion cases, internal moves, and compensation discussions, with no job-search framing required.
- Useful for internal moves? Yes, named explicitly and served by capture and retrieval.
- Useful for re-entry? Yes: the reconstruct route and Rosa's returner example.
- Useful after unexpected change? Yes: the second door and reconstruction.
- Does it avoid becoming a layoff product? Yes. Reconstruction is one door among several; the product serves the employed reader equally.
- Does it avoid becoming a resume product? Yes. It builds the record and hands off resume and application mechanics.
- Does it stop before destination-specific move decisions? Yes. The seam is explicit and enforced.
- Does it avoid implying translation controls recognition? Yes. It states plainly that general translation is not portability and portability is not recognition.
- Does the free Starter remain genuinely useful? Yes, unchanged, and the paid job is enlarged instead.
- Is the paid job materially larger? Yes, per Section 4.
- Does it feel worth $49 because of what the buyer establishes and can keep using, not page count? Yes. The value is the established record, the reconstruction path, the senior-work capture, and the maintainable home, not length.
- Could a free brag-document template substitute for the whole experience? No. No free template found combines permission discipline, senior non-technical judgment and prevention capture, corroboration readiness, and permission-safe reconstruction after access loss.
- Could AI recover the underlying facts if the reader never captured them? No, and the manuscript is built on that asymmetry: AI polishes wording, it cannot supply judgment, decisions, and prevention that were never recorded. That is why Clarify is the center and translation is kept lean.
