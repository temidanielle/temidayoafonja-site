How It Works
Capture. Clarify. Carry.
Three steps you return to whenever the work is fresh.
1
Capture
Catch the work in two minutes, before the details fade.
2
Clarify
Separate your part from the team's result, and name the judgment and the scope.
3
Carry
Put it in language an outsider can follow, then into a resume, an interview, or a promotion note.
Sixty minutes is a guide. Take it in one sitting or several.
