Who It Is For
For people who do the work and lose the proof.
The work was constant. You solved the problem, absorbed the lesson, and moved to the next thing before the last one had a name.
Before a performance review
Walk in able to account for the whole year, accurately.
Before a promotion case
Bring specific evidence of what changed because of your work.
Before an interview or a new resume
Have portable, specific language ready to reuse.
Before a reorg or a career move
Know what your work built and what can travel with you.
