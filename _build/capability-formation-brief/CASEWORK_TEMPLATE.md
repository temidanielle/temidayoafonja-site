# Casework Template

The Capability Formation Brief: case based working note template.

Version 1.0 draft for review. Not final until approved.

---

## What a case note is

A case based working note shows the reasoning, not generic advice. The reader is not paying to be told what to do. They are paying to watch a situation like theirs get read well, so they can read their own the same way next time. The value is the thinking made visible.

A case note is written once and read by everyone. It is never a private reply. The situation is anonymized, and the point is the method of the read, not the individual.

Every case considers, where relevant, the standing audit and the surrounding pressures: what travels, what does not, what the person can prove, what they must relearn, what the employer or manager or talent system is trying to de-risk, what evidence would materially change the decision, what constraint or boundary matters, and what the person should test or decide next.

## The editorial thinking template

This is the full set of headings to think through. It is not a fill-in-every-box form. Use the headings that carry the specific case, and leave out the ones that do not apply. A published case usually lands four to eight of these, not all ten. The template is for the reasoning behind the note, not a rigid shape imposed on the front of it.

### CASE
The situation, in the reader's own words, anonymized. One or two lines. This is the sentence a real reader would recognize as theirs.

### WHAT THE READER IS AFRAID MAY BE TRUE
The fear under the question. Usually some version of "the thing I built does not count" or "I have left it too late." Naming it is not agreeing with it. It sets what the read has to be honest about.

### WHAT I WOULD NOT ASSUME
The lazy reads to refuse. The places where the obvious interpretation is probably wrong, or at least unproven. This is where the note earns trust, by not taking the cheap answer.

### WHAT TRAVELS
The capability, judgment, and evidence that carries into the new context on its own.

### WHAT DOES NOT
The parts that are real but local, or that the new context will not accept on trust.

### WHAT CAN BE PROVED
Of what travels, the part that is currently inspectable. Evidence someone else could check, not just the person's own account.

### WHAT MUST BE RELEARNED
The capability the person genuinely has to earn again for the new context, separated cleanly from what they can carry.

### WHAT THE OTHER SIDE MAY BE TRYING TO DE-RISK
The decision from the deciding side. What is the manager, hiring panel, or talent system actually trying not to get wrong? Most "not ready" and "needs more exposure" messages are risk language, not verdicts on worth.

### WHAT EVIDENCE WOULD CHANGE THE READ
The specific, findable evidence that would move the decision. If nothing would, the read is stuck for a reason worth naming.

### THE BOUNDARY
The constraint that actually matters here. A permission line, a timing limit, a thing that cannot be proven without disclosing something the person may not disclose, or a genuine capability limit.

### WHAT I WOULD TEST NEXT
The next move. A test to run, a conversation to have, a piece of evidence to produce, or a decision condition to set. Never only reflection.

## House rules for case notes

1. Show the reasoning, including the parts that do not flatter the reader. A case note that only reassures is content, not casework.
2. Keep a genuine adverse read on the table when the evidence supports it. Sometimes the manager is right.
3. Anonymize fully. No employer, no identifying detail, no reconstructable specifics.
4. Do not promise outcomes. The read sharpens a decision. It does not guarantee a result.
5. Do not invent research, prevalence claims, or testimonials to support a read.
6. End with something the reader can do, tied back to the standing audit.
7. Cross file each case under the same five library categories used for the tools, so a reader working a problem finds the tools and the cases together.
