The Capability Formation Brief Tool Index
Paid decision tools

The library, so far

The first two instruments, with the problem each one answers.

Each tool is one to two pages, ten to twenty minutes, printable, and ends in a usable output rather than reflection prompts. They are filed by the reader problem they answer, not by publication date. Two of the planned ten are built here as pilots.

Readiness

Evidence Gap or Capability Gap?

A one-page read for being told you are not ready. Plain read, no new framework.

Separates four possibilities behind a "not ready" or "needs more exposure" message: a genuine capability gap, an evidence gap, an access gap, or more than one. It does not decide in the reader's favor by default.

Leaves you with

A named read (A, B, C, or D) and four lists: what to practice, what to prove, what access to ask for, and what to test next.

Two pages · about 10 to 15 minutes · printable
Role Decisions

The 3P Role Read

Practice, Proof, Portability. Uses the approved 3P language.

Reads whether the current role is still building the reader's future across three questions: what it is teaching them, what evidence it leaves behind, and what would carry if they left. A weak read is a signal to test, never an automatic instruction to resign.

Leaves you with

A three-line read, a named current trade, one capability to strengthen, and one 30 to 90 day test to run inside the role.

Two pages · about 10 to 20 minutes · printable
