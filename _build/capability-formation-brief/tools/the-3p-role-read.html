The 3P Role Read

Practice, Proof, Portability.

A role can keep you busy without building you. This read looks at your current role through three questions, one for what it is teaching you, one for what it is leaving behind, and one for what would carry if you left. It is a read, not a verdict. A weak result is a signal to test something inside the role, not an instruction to resign.

Three strengthening reads means the role is still building you. Keep it, and keep pushing on the strongest P.

A row of flat or eroding means the role is spending you faster than it is building you. That is worth acting on, but the action is a test, not an exit.

One weak P is a target, not a decision. Take it to page two.

From reading to testing

Every role is a trade. A good read is honest about both sides of it before deciding anything. Name the trade, pick the one capability worth strengthening, and set a single test you can run without leaving.

In one line: what this role gives me, and what it costs me, as it stands today.

3One 30 to 90 day test
