# NONCOMPLIANT — DO NOT USE

**These five files must never be uploaded, published, or used as launch artwork.**

They were supplied as Video 1 and Video 2 thumbnail candidates on 20 August 2026
and were rejected in the Three-Video Thumbnail System Audit. They are preserved
here as a record of what was rejected and why. Nothing here has been overwritten,
altered or deleted.

## Why they are rejected

They contain synthetic imagery presented as photographs of Temidayo.

| Ref | File | Finding |
|---|---|---|
| c3 | `NONCOMPLIANT_c3_YOUR_EXPERIENCE_COUNTS.png` | Centre polaroid shows **a different person** — different facial structure and hair — presented as Temidayo's career history |
| sheet | `NONCOMPLIANT_supplied_contact_sheet.png` | Panel 2 shows a **pose and setting that never existed**: caramel top, seated at a desk, finger to temple, full office behind. The real caramel photograph is a plain studio headshot with hands out of frame |
| sheet | `NONCOMPLIANT_supplied_contact_sheet.png` | Panel 2 props carry **pseudo-text** — letter-shaped marks spelling nothing, a signature of generative synthesis |
| c2 | `NONCOMPLIANT_c2_DONT_START_FROM_ZERO.png` | Insets show a **purple suit and a green shirt**. Neither garment exists in any photograph supplied at any point in this project |

The supplied contact sheet's own footer claims "No part of Temidayo's appearance
was generated or altered. All thumbnails use original photographs only." The
findings above contradict that claim directly.

## Additional problems

- All four candidates are **1672 x 941**, an aspect ratio of 1.7768. Not 16:9.
- Two of them (`c1`, `c3`) carry wording that is **not the locked launch copy**:
  WHAT STILL TRAVELS? and YOUR EXPERIENCE COUNTS are archived tests.
- None carries a version number, an approval marker, or any documentation.

- Video 2: `deliverables/video-2-slides/thumbnail/`
