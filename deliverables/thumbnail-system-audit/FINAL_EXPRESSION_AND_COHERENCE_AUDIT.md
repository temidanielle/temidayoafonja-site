## Step 3 — Expression audit

Assessed against the channel standard: the face should support the emotional
direction without miming the headline.

### Video 1 — Expression fit: **STRONG**

Open, warm smile with a direct gaze. The message is constructive — your experience
carries forward — and a genuine smile is the right carrier for it. It reads as
someone giving reassuring news, not as a creator reaction pose. It does not fight
the headline: "DON'T START FROM ZERO" is an encouragement, and the face encourages.

### Video 2 — Expression fit: **STRONG**

Closed-mouth, level, thoughtful, gaze slightly off-camera. This is exactly the
register the brief asked for and, importantly, it is **not** the broad celebratory
smile that would have fought the message. The diagnosis is carried by the
progression cue that climbs and then stops, not by the face — which is the correct
division of labour. The face says "I am considering this"; the graphic says "this
has stalled."

### Video 3 — Expression fit: **STRONG**

The same calm, level expression, which suits caution-without-alarm. There is no
mimed gesture and none was added. The pause signal is carried entirely by the
words and the gold underscore. A raised finger or palm would have pushed it toward
creator-reaction territory and away from the restrained credibility this channel
runs on — and no real photograph of that gesture exists in any case.

**Overly posed or reaction-driven:** none of the three. All are still portraits.
**Recognisable at 200 px:** all three. Verified visually at native 200 px; the face
occupies enough of the frame in each that the eyes and mouth resolve.

### The one thing worth knowing

**Videos 2 and 3 use the same photograph, and it shows.** Same expression, same
earring, same braid. At full mobile-feed width the two read as visibly the same
portrait.

Three reasons this is recorded as an observation rather than an action:

1. You explicitly permitted it. The rebuild brief said the Video 3 portrait may be
   used for Video 2.
2. **It is not material at the size that decides clicks.** At 200 px the layout
   inversion — portrait left against portrait right, navy-led against cream-led —
   separates them completely. Section D of the contact sheet shows this.
3. There is no better option in the real photo library. Only four usable
   photographs exist, and three of them are the same broad-smile wine session. A
   broad smile on Video 2 would fight its message, which is worse than a repeated
   face.

The only real fix is more photographs. If you ever shoot a second calm, level
portrait in different wardrobe, Video 2 is the thumbnail to spend it on.

## Channel recognition

Placed beside unrelated videos in a feed, would a viewer read these three as one
creator? **Yes.** Five things hold across all three: one typeface at one weight,
flush left in short stacked lines; one palette at identical hex values with gold
always an accent and never a field; one real portrait at 44 to 47% of the canvas;
one hard vertical seam marked by the same gold rule at the same weight; and one
restrained, unlabelled supporting graphic each.

What varies — which side the portrait sits on, cream-led against navy-led, and the
emotional register — is what stops three videos in one feed reading as a template.
