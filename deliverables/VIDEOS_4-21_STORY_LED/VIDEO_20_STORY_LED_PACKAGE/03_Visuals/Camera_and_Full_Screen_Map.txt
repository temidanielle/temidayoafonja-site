How to Return After a Career Break Without Starting at Zero

Every stretch of this video is marked. A row that says CAMERA is a stretch
where Temidayo carries the moment and no graphic should appear.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------

HOW TO READ THIS

  CAMERA        stay on Temidayo. Recognition, lived experience,
                tension, a reflective turn, or bringing the idea
                back to the viewer.
  FULL SCREEN   a true full-screen frame. No camera behind it and
                no shrinking it into a corner.

  Do not alternate mechanically. Several camera stretches in a row are
  correct where the script is telling a story.
