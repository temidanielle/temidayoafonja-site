THE VIEWER'S REASON TO WATCH

  OUTCOME
      Name the difference between the job accepted and the job being done,
      identify one cost and choose a specific next conversation or action.

  CURIOSITY
      Is the role still settling, or has it changed enough to require a
      different decision?

  WHY THIS METHOD
      Separating expectation, recurring reality, cost and choice makes a
      conversation more specific without assuming deception.

  OBSERVABLE TAKEAWAY
      One supported expectation, one recurring difference, one cost and
      one next action can be stated. Employer agreement or an easy exit is
      not promised.
