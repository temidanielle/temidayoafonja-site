### Overflow-check note

The geometry pass measures every rendered paragraph against the slide canvas and
reports anything crossing an edge. **Zero warnings of any kind were raised on the
final build**, so there is no distinction to draw between edge-only warnings and
real clipping on this deck. Full-slide background shapes are drawn at exactly
1920 x 1080 with no bleed, so they do not generate edge warnings either. All
audience-facing content sits inside the canvas.

Two findings were raised on earlier builds during this revision and were fixed
before delivery, both cosmetic rather than visible clipping:

1. Slide 9 body copy wrapped to three lines where two were intended. Re-broken to
   three deliberate lines.
2. Slide 2 timeline label boxes overlapped by 24 px at the box level, although the
   centred text never touched. Label boxes narrowed from 310 to 280 px so the boxes
   clear each other.

### Product boundary check

Slide 9 claims only that the Field Kit helps the buyer complete a private,
evidence-led career position assessment using the last 90 days of their actual
work. It does not claim to identify adjacent roles, map industries or employers,
produce a Career Portability Map, perform an AI Role Relevance Audit, or guarantee
title or compensation. Price is not mentioned on the slide or in the notes.
