### Scaling

| | Option A | Option B |
|---|---|---|
| Portrait region on the master | 1680 x 2160 | 1640 x 1660 |
| Crop taken from the source | 576 x 740 | 676 x 684 |
| Enlargement to reach the 4K master | 2.917x | 2.426x |

Scaling was required, and it is larger here than on Video 3 because the source
is 800 x 800. State it plainly: at the 3840 x 2160 master the photograph is
enlarged roughly 2.9x in Option A.

At the size YouTube actually serves, it is not enlarged at all. A thumbnail is
delivered at 1280 x 720 at most, where Option A's portrait region is 560 px
wide against a 576 px source crop — a downsample. The 4K master exists as an
archive, not as the delivered file.

Resizing used Lanczos resampling. No sharpening was applied.

### Why this photograph and not the others

The purple-outfit and green-shirt photographs called for in the original concept
**do not exist**. Every real photograph of Temidayo in this project shows the same
wine top. "Green" in `photo-headshot-green.png` names the sage backdrop, not a
shirt. Nothing was fabricated to fill that gap; the approved fallback was used
instead.

`photo-portrait-wine.png` (1122 x 1402) is higher resolution but was photographed
in a room with a window and patterned curtain behind her. The flat-backdrop
headshot separates from the layout far more cleanly at thumbnail size.

## Non-photographic elements added

Every element below is drawn vector-style. None is a photograph of anything, and
none represents measured data.

**Both options**
- Headline type: Montserrat ExtraBold, instanced from the repository variable font
- Deep navy field `#0F2346`, warm cream field `#F5F0E8`, gold rule `#C9A84C`
- The word ZERO carried in gold

**Option A**
- Four circular nodes on a thin rising line, ending in an arrowhead
- Node size increases left to right; the fourth is filled gold

**Option B**
- A navy band across the lower canvas with a gold edge
- Four ascending rectangular bars, the fourth filled gold
- A thin gold line across the bar tops, ending in an arrowhead

The four elements correspond to the real career path — Accounting and Audit,
Cybersecurity, People Strategy, Capability Formation — but they are **not
labelled**, because labels at this size become the miniature explanatory copy the
brief rules out. The graphic carries no numbers and implies no measurement.
