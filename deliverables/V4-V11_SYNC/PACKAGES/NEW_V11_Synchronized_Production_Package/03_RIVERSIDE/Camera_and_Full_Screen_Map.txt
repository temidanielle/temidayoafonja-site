               COST. What is the difference doing to my capability,
               evidence, compensation, or life? CHOICE. What can I
               clarify, negotiate, test, or decide next? That is a
               role-drift read. It gives you something more useful than
               “I hate this job” and something more honest than “I should
               just give it more time.”

SECTION 11  THE BOUNDARY

  PARA 1   MODE: FULL SCREEN
           ASSET FAMILY: NEW_V11_FS_10_THE_BOUNDARY

               NEW_V11_FS_10_THE_BOUNDARY.png
           EXACT TRIGGER:
               This framework does not tell you whether to leave.
               Sometimes the right choice is to stay because the drift is
               temporary or the actual role is better than the expected
               one. Sometimes you stay because leaving is not practical
               yet.

  PARA 2   MODE: FULL SCREEN
           ASSET FAMILY: NEW_V11_FS_11_YOUR_CONSTRAINTS
           STATES, IN REVEAL ORDER:
               NEW_V11_FS_11_YOUR_CONSTRAINTS.png
           EXACT TRIGGER:
               And sometimes the gap is large enough that you need to
               plan an exit. Your constraints are real. Compensation,
               health, caregiving, immigration, geography, timing, the
               market, and financial runway can all shape what is
               possible. The goal is not to force a dramatic decision. It
               is to make the decision from the job that actually exists.
