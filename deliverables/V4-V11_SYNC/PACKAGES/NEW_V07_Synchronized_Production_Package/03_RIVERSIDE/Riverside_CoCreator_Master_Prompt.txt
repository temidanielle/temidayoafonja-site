  OUTCOME
      Identify next-level work to practice or make visible, without
      treating every selection decision as a verdict on personal
      capability.

  CURIOSITY
      If the bigger role opened tomorrow, what could someone already point
      to for you?

  WHY THIS METHOD
      Naming the next decision or problem makes readiness more concrete
      than taking on unlimited extra work.

  OBSERVABLE TAKEAWAY
      A next-level decision or problem and a person who has seen relevant
      work can be named; access and organizational constraints are still
      assessed.
