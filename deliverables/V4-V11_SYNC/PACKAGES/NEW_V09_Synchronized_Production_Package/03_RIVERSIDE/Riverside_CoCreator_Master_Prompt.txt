THE VIEWER'S REASON TO WATCH

  OUTCOME
      Explain what the next role can use from the viewer’s experience,
      what supports that claim, and what still needs to be built.

  CURIOSITY
      Which part of the move becomes clearer when you name what does not
      travel?

  WHY THIS METHOD
      Sorting evidence and context-bound knowledge avoids the false choice
      between starting over and claiming everything transfers.

  OBSERVABLE TAKEAWAY
      The viewer can make a specific, bounded transfer statement with one
      proof example and one real learning need.
