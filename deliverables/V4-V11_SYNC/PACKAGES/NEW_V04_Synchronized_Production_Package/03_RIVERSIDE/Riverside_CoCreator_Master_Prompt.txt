THE VIEWER'S REASON TO WATCH

  OUTCOME
      Look at one AI-assisted task and explain what the tool did, what
      judgment remains yours, and where you still need practice.

  CURIOSITY
      If the tool gets faster, what are you still getting better at?

  WHY THIS METHOD
      Separating the task from the decisions inside it makes the learning
      question concrete.

  OBSERVABLE TAKEAWAY
      One task is mapped; one decision that remains yours and one learning
      need can be named. This is an exercise output, not proof of career
      growth.
