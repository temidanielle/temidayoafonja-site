  OUTCOME
      Contribute while learning the new context, build real proof and
      assess what the actual role is showing the viewer.

  CURIOSITY
      What should you prove in a context you do not yet fully understand,
      and what should the role show you?

  WHY THIS METHOD
      READ / TEST / PROVE / ROLE CHECK connects contribution with context
      and makes the evaluation two-way.

  OBSERVABLE TAKEAWAY
      A working page names what was learned, what traveled, what can now
      be demonstrated and what the role revealed. A giant win by day 90 is
      not required.
