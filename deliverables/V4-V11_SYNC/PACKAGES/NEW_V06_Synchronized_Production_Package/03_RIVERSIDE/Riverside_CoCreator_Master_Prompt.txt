THE VIEWER'S REASON TO WATCH

  OUTCOME
      Read a real posting well enough to decide whether it deserves a
      deeper look, separating the work, authority, proof and real gates.

  CURIOSITY
      What might the title conceal, and can an unclear title conceal
      substantial work too?

  WHY THIS METHOD
      Reading responsibilities and requirements separately avoids treating
      a title as the whole job.

  OBSERVABLE TAKEAWAY
      The viewer has a four-line role read, one relevant piece of their
      own evidence and a requirement to verify. The method does not
      predict hiring.
