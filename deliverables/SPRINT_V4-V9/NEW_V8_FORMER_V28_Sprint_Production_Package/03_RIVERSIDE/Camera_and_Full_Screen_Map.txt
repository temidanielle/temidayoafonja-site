      The day your access ends, your memory is still yours.
      Your work may not be.
      The dashboard is gone.
      The project folder is gone.
      The exact metric you improved is somewhere behind a login you cannot use anymore.
      And six months later, somebody asks:
      "What exactly changed because of your work?"

SECTION 2  THE PROBLEM
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  [ CAMERA ]  4 paragraphs, 43 words

SECTION 3  WHAT DISAPPEARS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  [ FULL SCREEN ]  NEW_V8_FS_02_FIVE_KINDS   (NEW)
      TRIGGER:  When access ends, you can lose five kinds of evidence.
      STATES:   NEW_V8_FS_02_FIVE_KINDS, NEW_V8_FS_02A_BASELINE, NEW_V8_FS_02B_SCOPE, NEW_V8_FS_02C_DECISION, NEW_V8_FS_02D_RESULT, NEW_V8_FS_02E_MECHANISM
      HOLD:     One activation per spoken pair. Hold the fifth through 'the details that turn a memory into proof.'

  [ CAMERA ]  11 paragraphs, 52 words
      The baseline.
      What was true before you started.
      The scope.
      How many people, teams, customers, locations, or systems were involved.
      The decision.
      What was actually yours to choose.
      The result.
      What changed.
      And the mechanism.
      How you know the result was real.
      Those are the details that turn a memory into proof.
