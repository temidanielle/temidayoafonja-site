What Disappears When Your Work Access Ends

Two to four full-screen moments at most, and only where the cut
needs them. Nothing here is required for the video to work.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  A notebook page with a short handwritten entry
      WHERE:  Under the ten-minute habit section.
      HOW:    Full screen. Handwriting only. No screen, no document, no file.

  A desk at the end of a working day
      WHERE:  Under the close.
      HOW:    Full screen, short. Nothing that depicts a system or a login.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------

DO NOT

  Never depict taking a file, a screenshot, a download, or any
  circumvention of access control. No fake internal system.

  No stock footage that implies a named employer.
  Substantive B-roll is full screen, never an overlay.
