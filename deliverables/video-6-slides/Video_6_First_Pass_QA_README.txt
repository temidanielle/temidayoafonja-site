JUDGMENT CALLS MADE
==============================================================

1. SLIDE MARKER PLACEMENT. The package gives approximate timings but not exact
   in-script insertion points. Markers were placed at the sentence where each
   slide's content is actually spoken, in numbered order. Slide 1 lands after
   the opening distinction and viewer promise, as instructed.

2. LAYOUT DEVICES DELIBERATELY DIFFER FROM VIDEO 5. Continuity is kept in
   palette, type, spacing and restraint. The devices are not cloned: Slide 2
   uses stacked bands rather than the Video 5 side-by-side column pair, Slide 4
   uses a muted-left / full-weight-right comparison with no panel, and Slide 6
   uses stacked definition rows. The numbered list on Slide 3 sits on navy
   where Video 5's sat on cream.

3. COLOUR RHYTHM. Section 5 specifies wording and reveals but not which ground
   each slide uses. Navy carries the structural and statement beats (1, 3, 5,
   7, 9, 11); cream carries the teaching detail (2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12).

4. TYPE SIZES. Supporting lines were set at the sizes established by the Video
   5 mobile-legibility pass, 22 to 25 pt, rather than the smaller sizes used in
   the Video 5 first pass. This avoids repeating that correction round.

5. LINE BREAKS. Headline line breaks are typographic choices to keep type large
   and mobile-readable. No wording was changed, added or removed.
