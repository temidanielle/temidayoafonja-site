## Two additional facts you should have before deciding

**1. Resolution.** Even with approval, this file cannot make a true 3840 x 2160
master. At 800 x 800 it is smaller than the 2160-pixel canvas height. A portrait
occupying 40 to 55 percent of a 4K canvas needs roughly 1500 to 2100 pixels of
height from the source. This file would need a 2 to 2.6 times upscale, which
shows on a large screen. The in-room frame at 1122 x 1402 is better but still
short of a clean 4K master.

**2. Expression.** The brief asks for a thoughtful, calm, consequential read, and
rules out celebration. This portrait is a broad, warm smile. It is a lovely
photograph and it worked well on the Video 1 career-path slide. Against the words
WAIT BEFORE YOU QUIT it would read as cheerful about quitting, which is the
opposite of the intended message.
