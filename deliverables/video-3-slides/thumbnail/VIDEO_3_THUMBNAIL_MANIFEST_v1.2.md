## Resampling record

The portrait region is 1800 x 2160 in Final A and 1760 x 2160 in Final B. The
crop taken from the source is 945 x 1134 for A and 924 x 1134 for B, so the
photograph is enlarged 1.905x to reach the 3840 x 2160 master.

This is not a quality loss at delivery. YouTube serves a thumbnail at 1280 x 720
at most, where the same portrait region is 600 px wide for A and 587 px for B.
The source crop supplies 945 px and 924 px respectively, so the file that
actually reaches a viewer is downsampled from the original, never enlarged.
The 4K master exists as an archive and for any future use at larger sizes.

Resizing used Lanczos resampling. No sharpening was applied.

## Photographic integrity

Applied: crop, Lanczos resize, placement.

Not applied, anywhere: AI face reconstruction, generative fill, beautification,
skin or feature retouching, synthetic extension of the frame, background
replacement, colour grading of the subject.

The photograph's own studio backdrop is carried through as shot. The visible
warmth difference between that backdrop and the cream panel in Final A is real,
and the gold rule on the seam is there to make it read as a deliberate edge.
