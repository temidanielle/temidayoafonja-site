WHAT v5.1 CHANGED

The spoken script was written fresh again for v5.1. It opens on where the
viewer already is - "You may already know" - then removes the thing they are
braced for: "I am not going to try to talk you into staying." The safety
boundary sits in the first minute. The three checks do not arrive until about
2:41. One brief truthful bridge was added - a role Temidayo had accepted was
rescinded by an acquisition before she started - to carry the point that
access can change without anybody choosing it. Video 1 owns the full
job-offer / Uber transition story; it is not retold here.
