==================================================

Use the useful structural learning from hook research:

- VISUAL HOOK

No fake suspense.

==================================================
H.I.T. FIRST-30-SECOND STANDARD
==================================================

H = HOOK
Immediately earns attention and matches title / thumbnail promise.

I = INTEREST
Gives the eye something meaningful to follow.
Do not default to 30 seconds of visually static talking head.

T = TRUST
Gives a concrete reason to believe Temidayo:
- lived career transition;
- real result;
- documented artifact;
- repeated organizational trust;
- specific before / after;
- honest failure / relearning;
- defensible career evidence.

Do NOT force a statistic into every opening.

First spoken sentence:
- one breath;
- natural;
- sounds like Temidayo.

Viewer payoff:
clear by approximately 15–20 seconds, no later than 30.

Treat the first 30 seconds as an AUDIOVISUAL UNIT.

Visual interest can come from:
- approved slide progression;
- text-on-screen;
- a real artifact;
- a real photo;
- a simple before / after;
- a career sequence;
- a meaningful contrast;
- a restrained editor cue.

Avoid decorative B-roll.
