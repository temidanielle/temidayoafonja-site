# Video 3 changelog: v1.0 to v1.1

**Version 1.1, Wednesday, August 19, 2026, America/Chicago**

A refinement pass, not a redesign. Two content changes, four supporting
documents, and a re-check of both publication blockers. Version 1.0 is
preserved unchanged, and Videos 1 and 2 were not touched.

## Changed

| # | Where | Before | After | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Slide 6, visible supporting line | "A resume bullet is not automatically portable evidence." | "A resume bullet does not automatically show what you can do." | "Portable evidence" is Capability Formation vocabulary. | 3 | Slide 4, speaker notes | No heuristic stated | Adds: "The test to say out loud: if you are not entitled to keep it, do not take it." | The deck notes did not carry a heuristic at all. This puts the approved one in front of the presenter at the moment it is needed. |

Nothing else changed. No slide moved, no geometry changed, no reveal structure
changed, no timing changed, and no other word of visible copy or speaker notes
was altered.

### Where the legal-team heuristic actually lived

Worth naming, because the instruction assumed it was in the presenter notes. It
was not. The v1.0 deck notes never contained it. It appeared only in the spoken
script and in the working sheet, so that is where it was replaced, and the
approved wording was added to the deck notes where it had been missing.

Because the change lives in the script, the script package was reissued at v1.1:
teleprompter, reading script, both Shorts and the working sheet. The v1.0 script
files are preserved.
