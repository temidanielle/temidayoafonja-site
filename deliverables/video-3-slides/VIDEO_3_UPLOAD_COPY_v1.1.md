## YouTube description

```
🧭 Career Decision Evidence Check: https://temidayoafonja.com/career-decisions

Most of the things to do before quitting your job are easiest while you are still
in the role. Records are reachable, systems are open, and the people who saw the
work are still nearby. The day after you give notice, a lot of that gets harder.

This video is not an argument for staying. It is three checks to run first.

01 Preserve the evidence. Keep what you are entitled to keep, and nothing you are
not.
02 Name what the work built. Problem, constraint, judgment, outcome, and then
where else that combination could matter.
03 Test the next move. A strong move uses something you have already proved and
requires you to build something genuinely new.

Then read what the evidence points to: leaving, repositioning inside, or building
a bridge first.

If your health or safety is at risk, or you are facing harassment or
discrimination, none of this is a reason to delay leaving. Act on that first.
```
