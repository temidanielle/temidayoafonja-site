## Open decisions, blocking the rest of the build

**1. The Claim needs Temidayo's confirmation.** Recommended:

> "I get brought in when the evidence is incomplete and a decision still has to
> be made."

It is a tightening of language already approved in the locked Video 4 script,
and it holds across audit, federal governance, privacy and cybersecurity, life
sciences and employee experience.

The research's own example, "I get organizations ready for scrutiny they can't
afford to fail", was deliberately not used as the primary. It is the better
sentence but it covers roughly half the evidence base: it fits audit,
governance and privacy, and does not fit being brought in to build a life
sciences capability function that did not yet exist. The script teaches that
trade-off out loud using both sentences, so if the scrutiny version is
preferred the demonstration section changes.

**2. The September 7 research document is not in this repository and was not
attached to the session.** These drafts were built from the brief's own
distillation of it plus the verified evidence base in `docs/claims-ledger.md`
and `about.html`.

**3. This collides with locked work, and the collision is not resolved here.**
The current Videos 4 and 5 are recorded as FINAL + LOCKED in
`SERIES_STATUS_TRACKER.md`, and the roadmap carries a standing rule that
Videos 1 to 5 are not to be rebuilt on research grounds. The September 7
instruction is newer and explicit, so drafting proceeded, but three things need
a decision: what happens to the locked packages, that the new Video 5 title
competes with the existing Video 4, and that the existing Video 4's Watch Next
points at the existing Video 5.
