VIDEOS 6, 7 AND 8  |  RUNTIME SOURCE-INTEGRITY AUDIT
==============================================================================

The question this audit answers is not whether the corrected master
says 'regular long-form'. It is whether the script inside it is the
full-depth script. A runtime estimate cannot answer that on its own,
because an estimate taken from a compressed file reports the
compression as a fact.

==============================================================================
VERDICT
==============================================================================

  V6, V7 and V8 are CASE C. Each corrected master carries about half
  the previously approved spoken script. This is not a measurement
  error, and it is not a full script whose finished runtime will
  simply run longer: a 575-word script cannot become an 11:30 to
  12:30 finished video without roughly six minutes of silence.

  NOTHING HAS BEEN EXPANDED. No approved speech has been edited,
  rewritten or padded. The corrected masters are untouched and their
  checksums are unchanged. This audit reports; it does not repair.

  V9 to V13 are CASE B. Their corrected masters match the previously
  approved masters to the word, so their speech-only estimates
  sitting below the printed finished-runtime targets is the normal
  difference between speech time and finished time. They are not
  padded and must not be.
