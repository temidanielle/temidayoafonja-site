  Recorded on a Shure microphone and the source may sit quieter than the
  final publishing level. Even out the speaking volume gently, keep natural
  dynamics, and improve dialogue consistency before the final loudness
  pass. No aggressive compression. No over-processed noise reduction, and
  no metallic or underwater artifacts. Preserve breaths and natural pauses
