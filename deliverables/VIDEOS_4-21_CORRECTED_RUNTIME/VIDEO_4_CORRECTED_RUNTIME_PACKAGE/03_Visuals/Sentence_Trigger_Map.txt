I Was First in 3 New Roles in 5 Years. Here's What I Stopped Doing.

Every graphic with the exact spoken sentence that cues it.
Each trigger was verified to exist inside a single thought block of
the locked master, so no cue is built from words that span two
takes.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------

 1. v4_01_title_new_work_not_new
    TRIGGER: Three times in five years, I stepped into a role that did not have a predecessor. Each time, the title was new to me. But the work underneath it was not completely new.
    MODE:    FULL SCREEN
    AFTER:   Return to camera on the next spoken line.

 2. v4_02_three_stops
    TRIGGER: What changed was how I judged whether I was ready. I stopped waiting for an exact title match. I stopped proving myself with a task list. And I stopped treating every learning gap like evidence that I was not ready.
    MODE:    FULL SCREEN
    AFTER:   Return to camera on the next spoken line.

 3. v4_03_three_questions
    TRIGGER: If you are looking at a role you have never held before, use three questions instead: What problem will I be trusted to solve? What can I already prove? What do I genuinely need to learn?
    MODE:    FULL SCREEN
    AFTER:   Return to camera on the next spoken line.

 4. v4_04_remove_the_title
    TRIGGER: A useful way to test this is to compare the old and new role without their titles.
    MODE:    FULL SCREEN
    AFTER:   Return to camera on the next spoken line.

 5. v4_05_task_list_vs_judgment
    TRIGGER: For unfamiliar work, look for evidence of judgment.
    MODE:    FULL SCREEN
    AFTER:   Return to camera on the next spoken line.

 6. v4_06_gap_is_not_a_verdict
    TRIGGER: A credible readiness case is not, 'I can already do every part of this.' It is, 'Here is what I can carry, here is the proof, and here is what I still need to learn.'
    MODE:    FULL SCREEN
    AFTER:   Return to camera on the next spoken line.

 7. v4_07_three_line_readiness_case
    TRIGGER: Take one role you have never held and write three lines.
    MODE:    FULL SCREEN
    AFTER:   Return to camera on the next spoken line.

 8. v4_08_what_it_does_not_guarantee
    TRIGGER: A strong readiness case does not guarantee that an employer will choose you.
    MODE:    FULL SCREEN
    AFTER:   Return to camera on the next spoken line.

 9. v4_09_cta
    TRIGGER: Being new to the title does not automatically mean being new to the work.
    MODE:    FULL SCREEN
    AFTER:   Return to camera on the next spoken line.

10. v4_10_watch_next
    TRIGGER: Being new to the title does not automatically mean being new to the work.
    MODE:    FULL SCREEN
    AFTER:   NO RETURN TO CAMERA. Watch Next is final.
