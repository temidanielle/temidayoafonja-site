VIDEO 13  |  SHORTS PRIORITY AND SOURCE MANIFEST
==============================================================================

A 30-Day Plan to Test Your Next Career Move

Six Shorts. Four Priority A, two Priority B.
Timings are arithmetic at 165 words per minute. Not timed reads.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------

PRIORITY A  v13_a1_stop_collecting_activity
    TITLE:  Stop collecting career activity
    WORDS:  88   ABOUT 32 SECONDS
    FIRST:  You have saved the jobs. Rewritten the headline. Maybe started a course.
    FINAL:  Choose one destination and write the hypothesis.
    SOURCE: V13 hook.

PRIORITY A  v13_a2_testable_destination
    TITLE:  Choose a destination specific enough to test
    WORDS:  74   ABOUT 27 SECONDS
    FIRST:  Something strategic is too vague to test.
    FINAL:  Write the hypothesis and what would change your mind.
    SOURCE: V13 setup.

PRIORITY A  v13_a3_ask_about_the_work
    TITLE:  Do not ask for reassurance. Ask about the work.
    WORDS:  76   ABOUT 28 SECONDS
    FIRST:  In the investigation week, do not ask for blanket reassurance about your potential.
    FINAL:  Ask those three, not whether you could do it.
    SOURCE: V13 days 8 to 14.

PRIORITY A  v13_a4_effort_is_not_evidence
    TITLE:  Do not confuse effort with evidence
    WORDS:  75   ABOUT 27 SECONDS
    FIRST:  At the end of the month, stop collecting activities and read the record.
    FINAL:  Write why you will continue, modify, or stop.
    SOURCE: V13 the evidence standard.

PRIORITY B  v13_b1_bounded_sample
    TITLE:  Try one bounded piece
    WORDS:  102   ABOUT 37 SECONDS
    FIRST:  In the third week, test one important part of the destination where a safe and relevant sample is possible.
    FINAL:  Set the time limit before you start.
    SOURCE: V13 days 15 to 21.

PRIORITY B  v13_b2_stop_is_a_good_result
    TITLE:  Stop is a real result
    WORDS:  82   ABOUT 30 SECONDS
    FIRST:  Continue does not mean resign tomorrow. It could mean a targeted conversation, an application, or a specific learning commitment now supported by evidence.
    FINAL:  Let the evidence choose, not the sunk time.
    SOURCE: V13 days 22 to 30.
