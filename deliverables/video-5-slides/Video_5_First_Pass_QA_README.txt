==============================================================
TECHNICAL SUBSTITUTIONS
==============================================================

ONE substitution was required.

The not-equal sign in the approved slide 1 copy — INTERNAL MOVE ≠ AUTOMATIC
GROWTH — cannot be typeset. No font in the repository contains U+2260:
not Montserrat, not DM Sans, not Cormorant Garamond.

Rather than substitute a different character, alter the approved wording, or
introduce a non-brand font, the mark is DRAWN as three editable vector shapes
in the muted gold. It is fully editable in PowerPoint and sits on-palette.

This follows the precedent approved for Video 4, where the directional arrow
glyph was drawn for the same reason.

No other substitution was made. No palette value was invented.

   for each slide but does not mark exact in-script insertion points. Markers
   were placed at the sentence where each slide's content is actually spoken,
   in the numbered order, matching the Section 5 timings. Slide 1 lands on the

   each slide uses. The three question slides (3, 5, 7) all use the deep navy
   ground so they read as one series; teaching detail slides alternate to warm
   cream. This follows the Video 4 pattern.

3. LINE BREAKS. Headline line breaks are typographic choices made to keep type
   large and mobile-readable. No wording was changed, added or removed.

4. INTERNAL LABEL EXCLUDED. The label "VISUAL TEACHING SYSTEM" appears at the
   end of Section 4 in the source package. It is not spoken content, so it is
   excluded from both script files. This matches the correction applied to
   Video 4.
