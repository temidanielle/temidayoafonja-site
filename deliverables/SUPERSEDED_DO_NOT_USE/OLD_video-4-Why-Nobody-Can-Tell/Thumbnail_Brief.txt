ARTWORK DIRECTION

  No props. No arrows. Real photography.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT TO AVOID
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  The research flags one specific trap on this line. THEY CAN'T READ YOU
  invites a literal visual: reading glasses, a document, a magnifier, a
  resume
  held up to the light. Resist all of it. The strongest version is a human
  misreading, not a document being read.

  Also avoid: any cat imagery, in any form. The nine lives line is verbal
  only and
  appears once in the script.

  Do not use in title or thumbnail: non-linear, portability, capability
  formation,
  transferable skills, reinvention. The research records direct evidence of
  these
  framings failing, at 56 to 629 views.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
NOTE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  The third option is the most shareable of the three and the one senior
  professionals send to each other. It is listed for completeness but it
  conflicts with the production brief, which rules that stating “interesting
  background is never a compliment” is too absolute. If it is ever tested,
  the video's own cold open would need rewording to match, since the script
  deliberately allows that the phrase is sometimes genuine.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
BRAND
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Deep navy #112345, warm cream, muted gold, real Temidayo photography.
  Three to five words, high contrast, readable at 120 pixels wide. The
  thumbnail is recognition, tension or future identity. It is not a second
  title.
