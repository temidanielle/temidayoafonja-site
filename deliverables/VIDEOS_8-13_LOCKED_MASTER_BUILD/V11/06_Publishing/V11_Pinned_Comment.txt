Choose one recurring output from your job. Not your whole profession.

Make three columns and put a verification question under each:

PRODUCE. What can an approved tool help produce?
INTERPRET. What must be established before anyone relies on it?
DECIDE. What decision does it inform, and who owns that decision?

Then put your own contribution on the map honestly. Separate what you already do from what you could learn and what belongs to another qualified role.
