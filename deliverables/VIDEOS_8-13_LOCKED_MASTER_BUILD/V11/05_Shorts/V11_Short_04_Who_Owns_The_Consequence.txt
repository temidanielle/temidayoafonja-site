RECORDING SCRIPT

When someone asks whether a tool means you can cut the team, the answer is not another prettier report.
It is a decision process.
Who is authorized to make the change?
Which risks must be accepted or reduced?
What alternatives are available?
What would trigger a review or reversal?
Who will monitor what happens after the decision?
Those are organizational responsibilities. A tool producing a recommendation does not automatically assign them to anyone.
And do not take responsibility for decisions you cannot influence. If your role is being redesigned, authority, access, and support need to be part of that conversation too.

FINAL SPOKEN LINE
Authority, access, and support need to be part of that conversation too.
