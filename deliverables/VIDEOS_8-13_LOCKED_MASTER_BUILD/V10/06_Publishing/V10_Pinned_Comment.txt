Start with one entry, not your whole year.

CAPTURE. What was the problem? What was my role? What judgment did I contribute? What result am I allowed to describe?

QUALIFY. Put one label beside the result: confirmed, qualified, or not yet verified.

MAKE IT RETRIEVABLE. Name it by the problem, not the project code. Add the period, a few plain-language tags, and the qualification you need to preserve.

When the entry feels vague, ask whether the missing piece is context, judgment, attribution, or evidence.

Keep only your own account and information you are permitted to retain. If you are unsure what you can keep, leave it out and get appropriate guidance.
