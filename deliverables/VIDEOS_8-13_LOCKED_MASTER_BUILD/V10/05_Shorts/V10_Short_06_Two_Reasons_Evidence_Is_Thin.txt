RECORDING SCRIPT

There are two very different reasons an example may be missing.
You may have done the work and failed to capture it clearly.
Or you may never have had the opportunity to own the decision, access the result, or build that part of your range.
Do not solve both problems by writing a stronger sentence.
The first needs reconstruction.
The second needs an honest account of the experience you do and do not have.
If a result was never shared with you, say what you can support about your contribution. Do not fill the gap with what probably happened.
And if a layoff has already happened, do not hear this as you missed your chance.

FINAL SPOKEN LINE
Do not hear this as you missed your chance.
