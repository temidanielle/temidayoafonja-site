RECORDING SCRIPT

Before you build any record of your work, one boundary.
Access to a document does not mean you are entitled to keep it.
Removing a company name does not automatically make restricted material yours to retain.
No forwarding internal emails. No downloading dashboards. No copying customer information. No collecting employee records.
This exercise is about your own high-level account and information you are permitted to retain.
If you are unsure what you can keep or disclose, leave it out and get appropriate guidance.
A personal device is not a permission slip. The first question is whether the information belongs there at all.
Keep an account of your contribution, not a copy of your employer's files.

FINAL SPOKEN LINE
Keep an account of your contribution, not a copy of your employer's files.
