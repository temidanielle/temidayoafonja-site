Choose one thing people now take for granted because it works.

Then write four sentences:

1. Before this work, this was missing.
2. My part was this.
3. The judgment involved this.
4. What changed was this, and this is how I know.

Now remove the employer name and the internal acronyms. Can a stranger still tell what the starting problem was, what you contributed, and what your evidence supports?

Keep your own account and information you are permitted to retain. Nothing confidential belongs in this exercise.
