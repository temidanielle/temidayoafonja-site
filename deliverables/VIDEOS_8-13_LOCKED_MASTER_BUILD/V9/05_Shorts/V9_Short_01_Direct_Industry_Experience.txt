RECORDING SCRIPT

You can be very experienced and still read one sentence in a job description that makes you feel like a beginner again.
Direct industry experience required.
You know you have done difficult work. You know you are not starting your career over.
But a new industry may not count your experience exactly the way your current one does.
So do not make either mistake.
Do not say, I can already do all of this.
And do not say, none of my experience counts here.
Separate three things instead. Capability. Context. Credentials.
What can you already prove? What changes in this setting? And what do you genuinely need before the door is open?

FINAL SPOKEN LINE
What do you genuinely need before the door is open?
