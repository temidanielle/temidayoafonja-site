RECORDING SCRIPT

A new industry may value some of your experience without preserving all of your current scope, pay, authority, or status.
That is not a universal rule. It is something to investigate before you commit.
So ask four questions.
What are you gaining?
What are you giving up?
For how long?
And what evidence supports the opportunity rather than just the hope?
That last one matters most, because hope is easy to collect and evidence is not.
Being new to an industry is not the same as being new to every problem inside it.
