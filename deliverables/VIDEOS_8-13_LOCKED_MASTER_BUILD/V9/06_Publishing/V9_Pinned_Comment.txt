Choose one destination role. Not five industries at once.

Write three headings and, under each, separate what you can support now from what you still need answered:

CAPABILITY. This role needs someone to handle this problem. My evidence is this example.
CONTEXT. What changes when the setting changes, and how big is each difference really?
CREDENTIALS. Confirmed requirement, preferred qualification, or still unclear?

Then make one move that reduces uncertainty. Verify a requirement. Ask about a difficult decision in the role. Find the strongest example from your own work.
