Write these four lines privately. You do not have to share them anywhere.

1. What must hold for now?
2. What part of the current situation can I influence safely?
3. What one question would make the next option clearer?
4. When will I review this again?

The review point can be a date or a condition. It does not need to be a resignation deadline.

If your capacity is extremely limited, choose only the first line and the review point. That is a deliberate choice, not a failure.

And if health, safety, harassment, discrimination, or another urgent issue is involved, appropriate support comes first.
