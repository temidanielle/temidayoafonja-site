A Constraint Is Not a Conclusion
Priority A
On-screen hook: WHAT CAN CHANGE NOW?
Source section: HOOK | I cannot leave yet is not the same as nothing can change
97 words; estimated 35 seconds. Estimate until recorded.

FIRST SPOKEN LINE
Just leave is easy advice when nobody has asked what your paycheck has to cover.

RECORDING SCRIPT

Just leave is easy advice when nobody has asked what your paycheck has to cover.
Maybe you need the income. Maybe benefits matter. Maybe caregiving, immigration status, or the timing of your life makes a quick exit unrealistic.
So start with a more accurate sentence.
I cannot leave yet. That is a constraint.
Nothing can change. That is a conclusion.
They are not the same thing.
You do not need a second full-time job called escaping the first one.
You need to protect what must hold, put boundaries where you can, and prepare one possible next step.

FINAL SPOKEN LINE
Protect what must hold, put boundaries where you can, and prepare one possible next step.
