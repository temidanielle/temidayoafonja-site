Turn One Accomplishment Into Career Proof

Two to four full-screen moments at most, and only where the cut
needs them. Substantive B-roll is full screen. Nothing here is
required for the video to work.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Hands writing four lines on paper
      WHERE:  Under the CTA, or under the sentence about the second line taking the longest.
      HOW:    Full screen. The written words must not be legible as a different example.

  A whiteboard being wiped clean
      WHERE:  Only if the cut between the judgment beat and the proof beat needs air.
      HOW:    Full screen, brief.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  No stock footage of an office that implies a named employer.
  No screen recording of a live job board.
  No filmed posting page: the postings appear as built cards
  from the preserved capture, not from a page reopened later.
