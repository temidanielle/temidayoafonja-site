SECTION 2  THE PROMISE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  [ CAMERA ]  5 paragraphs, 70 words
      Give me one accomplishment and I want you to rebuild it in four lines:
      Problem before. What was mine to decide. Judgment. Proof and how I know.
      You can do this in about 10 minutes once you know the questions.
      And the point is not to make the story sound bigger.
      The point is to make it easier for someone who was not there to judge what you actually carried.

SECTION 4  LINE 1: PROBLEM BEFORE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  [ CAMERA ]  8 paragraphs, 70 words
      First, what was true before you stepped in?
      This is easy to skip because you remember the whole story.
      The reader does not.
      Without the before state, a number has no scale.
      An 18 percent improvement could be huge or almost meaningless depending on what was happening be...
      So name the problem plainly.
      What was broken? What was inconsistent? What could the organization not do?
      Two sentences is usually enough.


------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SECTION 5  LINE 2: WHAT WAS MINE TO DECIDE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  [ CAMERA ]  7 paragraphs, 68 words
      This is the line I want you to spend the most time on.
      We are used to answering, "What were you responsible for?"
      Then we give a title or a list of duties.
      Try a harder question:
      What was mine to decide?
      And if your first answer is, "Honestly, I did not own much authority. I mostly executed," do not...
      Execution still contains decisions.

  [ FULL SCREEN ]  V23_FS_07_EXECUTION_CONTAINS_CHOICES   (COPY UPDATE)
      TRIGGER:  What did you sequence? What did you escalate? What did you challenge? What did you recommend? What tradeoff did you put in front of someone else?
      STATES:   V23_FS_07_EXECUTION_CONTAINS_CHOICES
      HOLD:     Hold through the five questions, then cut to camera for the sentence about the postings.

  [ CAMERA ]  1 paragraph, 14 words
      Several senior roles in the postings I studied clearly described support or execution postures.

  [ FULL SCREEN ]  V23_FS_08_AUTHORITY_BOUNDARY   (COPY UPDATE)
      TRIGGER:  So this is not about pretending you had more authority than you did.
      STATES:   V23_FS_08_AUTHORITY_BOUNDARY
      HOLD:     Cut on 'So this is not about pretending' and hold through 'the decisions that were genuinely yours.'

  [ CAMERA ]  1 paragraph, 10 words
      It is about finding the decisions that were genuinely yours.

SECTION 9  THE CATCH
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

      There is one catch.
      The same accomplishment will not matter in the same way everywhere.
      A new employer may care most about your judgment.
      Another may care about the measurement.
      Another may care that you delivered without formal authority.
