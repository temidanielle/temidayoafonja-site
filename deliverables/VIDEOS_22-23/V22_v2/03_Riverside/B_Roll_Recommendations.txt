VIDEO 22  |  B-ROLL RECOMMENDATIONS
==============================================================================

Decode a Job Description in 10 Minutes

Two to four full-screen moments at most, and only where the cut
needs them. Nothing here is required for the video to work.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Reading on a laptop, over the shoulder, no screen legible
      WHERE:  Under the promise section only. The postings themselves are shown as built cards, never as a filmed screen.
      HOW:    Full screen if used at all. Never behind a framework card.

  Hands and a notebook, writing four short words
      WHERE:  Under the CTA, or under the method recap in the boundary section.
      HOW:    Full screen. No text overlay.

  A quiet desk at the end of a working day
      WHERE:  Under the boundary section, where the video admits what the method cannot do.
      HOW:    Full screen, short. Do not use it to soften the boundary.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  No stock footage of an office that implies a named employer.
  No screen recording of a live job board.
  No filmed posting page: the postings appear as built cards
  from the preserved capture, not from a page reopened later.
