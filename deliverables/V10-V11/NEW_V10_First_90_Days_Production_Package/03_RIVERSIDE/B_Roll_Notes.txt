Your First 90 Days in a New Job: What Really Matters

Two to four full-screen moments at most, and only where the cut
needs them. Nothing here is required for the video to work.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Hands and a notebook, writing a short list
      WHERE:  Under the READ section, where the four things are being named.
      HOW:    Full screen, brief. No text legible, and nothing that implies a specific employer.

  A quiet desk in an unfamiliar room, nobody in frame
      WHERE:  Under WHY THIS FEELS STRANGE, on the line about not knowing which meeting matters.
      HOW:    Full screen, short. No office stock energy, no people, no signage.

  A doorway or corridor before a meeting
      WHERE:  Under THE CONVERSATION I WOULD HAVE, if the cut needs air before the manager questions.
      HOW:    Full screen, brief. Never behind the questions card.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------

DO NOT

  No office stock energy, no people in frame, no signage, no
  identifiable workplace. Nothing that reenacts a manager conversation,
  and nothing that implies a specific employer.

  No stock footage that implies a named employer.
  Substantive B-roll is full screen, never an overlay.
