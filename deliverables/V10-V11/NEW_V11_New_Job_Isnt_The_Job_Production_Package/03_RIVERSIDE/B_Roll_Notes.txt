What to Do When Your New Job Isn’t the Job You Accepted

Two to four full-screen moments at most, and only where the cut
needs them. Nothing here is required for the video to work.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  Two sheets of paper side by side on a desk
      WHERE:  Under the EXPECTED and ACTUAL sections, where the two columns are being described.
      HOW:    Full screen, brief. No text legible. It suggests the exercise without pretending to be the viewer's own.

  A window or a walk, mid-afternoon, nobody in frame
      WHERE:  Under THE BOUNDARY, where the script says the framework does not tell you whether to leave.
      HOW:    Full screen, short. Nothing that reads as resignation or as defeat.

  Hands closing a notebook
      WHERE:  Under the CLOSE, if the cut needs a breath before Watch Next.
      HOW:    Full screen, brief. Never behind the role-drift read card.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------

DO NOT

  Nothing that frames the employer or the manager as a villain: no
  closed door read as a rejection, no packed box, no resignation
  imagery. Nothing that implies a specific employer.

  No stock footage that implies a named employer.
  Substantive B-roll is full screen, never an overlay.
