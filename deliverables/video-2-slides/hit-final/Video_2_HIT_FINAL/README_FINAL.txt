WHAT v5.0 CHANGED

A genuine editorial rebuild, not a patch. The script was written fresh under
the belonging-first register: the viewer's situation and the private question
underneath it come first, then the explicit reassurance that being valuable
here is real value, then the mechanism by which value quietly stops
travelling. The three tests do not arrive until about 2:20. The video now
ends on the habit of checking rather than on a plan to leave.
