What You Must Relearn When You Change Industries

Six Shorts. Four Priority A, two Priority B.
Timings are arithmetic at 165 words per minute. Not timed reads.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------

PRIORITY A  v21_a1_the_room_is_different
    TITLE:  You can still do the work
    WORDS:  80   ABOUT 29 SECONDS
    FIRST:  You made the industry change.
    FINAL:  Make the inventory for one destination, not the whole industry.
    SOURCE: V21 hook.

PRIORITY A  v21_a2_licensing_is_not_a_mindset
    TITLE:  A licensing requirement is not a mindset issue
    WORDS:  88   ABOUT 32 SECONDS
    FIRST:  If a role requires a license, a registration, or a qualification you do not hold, adjacent experience does not erase that requirement.
    FINAL:  Check the binding requirements before anything else.
    SOURCE: V21 regulation and credentials.

PRIORITY A  v21_a3_five_layers
    TITLE:  Five layers to relearn
    WORDS:  75   ABOUT 27 SECONDS
    FIRST:  When you change industries, sort the relearning into five layers.
    FINAL:  Reorder your list by what blocks you first.
    SOURCE: V21 the five-layer inventory.

PRIORITY A  v21_a4_easiest_layer_trap
    TITLE:  Do not prepare for the easiest layer
    WORDS:  76   ABOUT 28 SECONDS
    FIRST:  Systems and tooling are often the most visible gaps, because they have names.
    FINAL:  Give the harder layers real preparation time.
    SOURCE: V21 systems and tooling.

PRIORITY B  v21_b1_has_anyone_tried_this
    TITLE:  Has anyone tried this before?
    WORDS:  76   ABOUT 28 SECONDS
    FIRST:  There is knowledge in a new organization you cannot read your way into.
    FINAL:  Ask it before you propose anything.
    SOURCE: V21 relationships and internal history.

PRIORITY B  v21_b2_read_or_practice
    TITLE:  Some of it you read. Some of it you practice.
    WORDS:  77   ABOUT 28 SECONDS
    FIRST:  Some parts of a new industry can be read. Requirements, documentation, domain facts, formal processes.
    FINAL:  Start the readable items this week.
    SOURCE: V21 read it or practice it.
