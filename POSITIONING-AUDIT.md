# Website Positioning Audit and Implementation

**temidayoafonja.com — offer differentiation, "coaching" language, and the Executive Capability Portfolio**
Date: 2026-07-28 · Scope: homepage, Work With Me, individual and organizational offers, framework, about, navigation, CTAs · Companion to the earlier platform audit in `AUDIT-REPORT.md`

---

## Final ruling, up front

The website architecture is **stronger than a rebuild**. The visual identity, the Capability Formation IP, and the voice are already premium and coherent. The problem is **not** design, navigation, or page structure. It is a **combination of three things, in this order**:

1. **Offer hierarchy and buyer segmentation.** The individual work sits as offer 4 of 4, after three organizational offers, with no pathway of its own. Individuals are an afterthought in the layout, not a pathway.
2. **Naming.** "Individual Capability Coaching" makes substantive assessment work sound like conventional career coaching, and it quietly caps the price. Worse, the site already describes **two different individual things** under overlapping language: a 90-day "coaching" engagement and a 60-minute "Capability Session." A buyer cannot tell which one is the offer.
3. **Calls to action.** The individual CTA is a "waitlist" that links to the **book** page (`book.html`), a genuine mismatch, and "waitlist" signals an offer that is not really open.

The smallest viable adjustment fixes all three without touching the framework, the palette, or the organizational credibility. That is what has been implemented. The Executive Capability Portfolio has been introduced **as a discreet, by-request pilot**, not as a homepage centerpiece and not as an established, priced product.

## PHASE 1 — Positioning audit

**First 10 seconds (homepage).** A reader sees "Building capability is a design problem," the eyebrow "Organizational Capability Architect," and an 18-years-across-Deloitte-PwC-EY credential row. The message that lands is: *this is a serious organizational/enterprise capability practice, built on a named framework, with a diagnostic as the front door.* That is a strong, accurate first impression, and it should be preserved.

**Primary buyer.** Unambiguously the **organizational leader** (CHRO, CPO, COO). Three of the four "Work With Me" offers are organizational; the individual offer is last and tagged in a different color. The site does **not** read as "executive career coaching," which is the outcome the brief wanted to protect. Good.

**Do individual and organizational offers feel connected?** Partially. The framework page and diagnostic explicitly serve both ("twelve statements for individuals, ten questions for organization leaders"), which is the connective tissue and works well. But in the offer layout the individual work is bolted on rather than presented as *the same framework applied to one career.* The connection is stated in theory and lost in the hierarchy.

**Is the offer hierarchy clear?** No. Four offers presented as a flat "four ways to engage the framework" grid mix two buyers and two price tiers with no grouping. There is no "for individuals / for organizations" fork on the offer surface, and the individual tier's two rungs (Capability Session vs. 90-day coaching) contradict each other.

**Where "coaching" weakens, helps, or confuses.**
- *Weakens:* "Individual Capability Coaching" positions substantive assessment (capability translation, portability analysis, decision architecture, a written portfolio) as pep-talk coaching. It undersells the work and caps the price. A $5,000–$12,500 engagement cannot credibly be called "coaching" in this frame.
- *Confuses:* the same section promises "biweekly 1:1 sessions over 90 days," while the diagnostic and the "if you are ready" box promise a "60-minute Capability Session." Two offers, one label.
- *Helps:* nowhere meaningfully. There is no lower-tier, high-volume individual offer where "coaching" is the honest, expected word, because the accessible individual products are self-serve (diagnostic, Field Kit, field guide, Substack), not coaching.

**Where the Executive Capability Portfolio naturally belongs.** As the **premium rung of the individual pathway**, sitting **above** the accessible individual path and **beside** the organizational offers, reached **by request**. It does not belong on the homepage as a centerpiece and it does not replace the individual path.

**Does the site support a $5,000–$12,500 private advisory offer today?** **No, not as an established product.** There are no attributed individual testimonials, no named executive outcomes (case studies are deliberately anonymized patterns), and the individual offer is currently framed as either free-adjacent (a Capability Session from the diagnostic) or as "coaching." Dropping a fully-priced $12.5k product onto that foundation would read as a stretch. The site *can* support a **by-request private engagement** introduced as a founding-cohort pilot, with pricing disclosed in conversation. That is the credible altitude right now.

**Language that creates a credibility gaps.** "Waitlist Open" and "Limited Spots" on an offer that then links to the book page reads as aspirational rather than real. "Personal capability architecture" is abstract where the work is concrete. These undercut trust.

**Pages or sections that compete.** The individual offer (`work.html` #4), the "Capability Session" (in the "ready to start" block and in `diagnostic.html`) describe overlapping-but-different individual engagements and compete for the same click. Consolidating them into one pathway removes the collision.

**Do CTAs match offer maturity?** No. Organizational offers correctly use "send a note / request pricing." The individual offer used "Join the Waitlist" pointing at the book. The mature move for a premium individual offer is "Request a private conversation," not a waitlist.

**Sections that should remain unchanged (do not touch):**
- The hero: "Building capability is a *design* problem" and the credential row.
- The entire **Framework** page (Density / Optionality / Alumni Capital, the equation, the Four States matrix).
- The **diagnostic** as the universal front door and its "twelve statements / ten questions" line.
- The **About** narrative and career timeline.
- The **organizational** offers 01–03 on Work With Me (Diagnostic, Architecture Advisory, Speaking).
- The palette, typography, whitespace, mobile nav, and footer.

## PHASE 2 — Recommended offer architecture

Keep **Capability Formation** as the category spine. Present the offers as **two pathways**, individual and organizational, each an application of the framework.

### For Organizations (primary buyer, unchanged)
| | Buyer | Problem | Outcome | Delivery | Hierarchy | Primary CTA |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Capability Formation Diagnostic** | CHRO/CPO/COO | Can't see where capability compounds or leaks | Scored report, quadrant placement, 90-day plan | 4–6 week engagement | Entry (org) | Send a note |
| **Capability Architecture Advisory** | CHRO/CPO | Structural redesign beyond a plan | Role architecture, mobility, Alumni Capital systems | Retained | Premium (org) | Request pricing |
| **Speaking & Executive Sessions** | Boards, offsites, peer groups | Need the framework to land with leaders | Customized session + leave-behind | Per engagement | Reach/credibility | See topics |

### For Individuals (the restructured pathway)
| | Buyer | Problem | Outcome | Delivery | Hierarchy | Primary CTA |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Individual Capability Formation** | Mid-senior professionals | Don't know what they've built or what it's worth | Diagnostic → Field Kit → field guide → a Capability Session with a written inventory | Self-serve + one working session | Accessible (entry) | Take the diagnostic |
| **Executive Capability Portfolio** | Senior executives in a consequential transition | Capability is real but unorganized; a high-stakes decision is close | Capability assessment, translation, decision architecture, portability analysis, transition strategy, a **written portfolio** | Private, by request, founding cohort | Premium (individual) | Request a private conversation |

**Relationship between offers.** Everything begins with the diagnostic. Individuals move down a self-serve path; senior executives with a consequential decision can request the private portfolio. Organizations run the same framework at workforce scale. One framework, two buyers, two price ladders.

**Ruling on the Work With Me structure:** not "three offers," not a flat four. Use **two labeled pathways (Individuals / Organizations)**. This is the structure that removes the buyer collision while keeping organizational credibility first.

### Where the Executive Capability Portfolio should sit — the explicit ruling
- **Replace Individual Capability Coaching?** No. "Coaching" is retired, but the Portfolio does not replace the *accessible* individual path; that path still needs to exist for the many.
- **Sit above it?** Yes — it is the premium rung of the individual pathway.
- **Sit beside the organizational offers?** Yes, as a peer in seniority, reached differently (by request).
- **Begin as a discreet pilot page?** **Yes. This is the chosen option.** A dedicated page (`executive-portfolio.html`), `noindex`, not in the main nav, linked only from a restrained strip on Work With Me.
- **Remain off the public site until validated?** No — a by-request pilot is lower-risk than invisibility and lets real interest validate the offer. But it is deliberately kept off the homepage and out of search.

## PHASE 3 — Language recommendations

Voice preserved throughout: editorial and restrained, concrete before abstract, no em dashes, no "not X, it's Y," none of unlock/leverage/navigate/game-changing/transformative, no luxury inflation, no hard sell, no guaranteed outcomes.

| Term | Ruling | Why current is insufficient | What the new term communicates | Residual risk | Clear to a senior exec? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Individual Capability Coaching** | **Replace** → *Individual Capability Formation* (path) + *Executive Capability Portfolio* (premium) | Sounds like generic career coaching; caps price; collides with "Capability Session" | Same framework applied to one career; assessment, not pep talk | "Formation" is slightly abstract; mitigated with a concrete subhead | Portfolio: yes. Formation: with the subhead, yes |
| **Coaching** | **Retire as a label** | No honest home for it — the accessible individual products are self-serve, not coaching | Removes the conventional, conversational read | Someone may still ask "is this coaching?"; the copy answers plainly | n/a |
| **Work With Me** | **Retain (nav), restructure (page)** | Label is fine; the page structure was the problem | Human, low-friction entry to two pathways | Slightly informal for enterprise, but consistent with the voice | Yes |
| **Career transition** | **Reframe** → *consequential transition / executive transition strategy* | "Career transition" alone reads as outplacement | Seniority and stakes | "Transition" is broad; qualified by "consequential" | Yes |
| **Capability architecture** | **Retain (org), reduce (individual)** | Fine for organizations; abstract for a person | Structural, system-level for orgs | Keep it out of individual copy in favor of "assessment/portfolio" | Yes (org context) |
| **Executive advisory** | **Adopt as descriptor** → *private advisory* | Not currently used for individuals | Senior, discreet, bespoke | Could imply a big firm; "by request, small cohort" corrects it | Yes |
| **Diagnostic** | **Keep** | Well-established front door | Rigor, evidence, a real instrument | None | Yes |
| **Portfolio** | **Adopt** → *capability portfolio* | The written-deliverable framing was missing | A substantive artifact you keep, not call notes | Not "investment portfolio" — context makes it clear | Yes |
| **Next chapter** | **Continue to avoid on marketing surfaces** | Cliché; not currently on the site | — | Used once in prose on the pilot page in its literal sense, not as a tagline | — |
| **Organizational capability** | **Keep** | Core and accurate | The institutional body of work | None | Yes |

## PHASE 5 — Code implementation plan

**Stack:** static HTML per page, one shared `styles.css` (design tokens, nav, footer, buttons, motion), `nav.js` (accessible hamburger that clones `.nav-links`), Netlify hosting with `netlify.toml` redirects and Netlify Functions for the diagnostic. Copy lives inline in each page's HTML; page-specific CSS lives in each page's `<style>` block. Forms post to a single Formspree endpoint with a `source` tag. Analytics via Plausible.

**Files modified / added and why:**
1. `index.html` — homepage service card 04 relabel + CTA fix (individual pathway consistency).
2. `work.html` — reframe individual offer 04, fix mislinked CTA, add discreet Executive pilot strip, fix pre-existing mobile grid overflow.
3. `executive-portfolio.html` *(new)* — the by-request pilot page, reusing the design system.
4. `netlify.toml` — clean `/executive-portfolio` rewrite for private sharing.
5. `robots.txt` — disallow the pilot page (keeps it discreet; it is also `noindex` and absent from `sitemap.xml`).

**Reusable components:** nav, footer, `.btn-primary`/`.btn-secondary`, `.eyebrow`, `.principles-link-strip` (reused for the pilot strip), the Formspree submit pattern — all reused, nothing forked.

**Duplicated copy to keep consistent:** the "Individual Capability Coaching" label appeared in both `index.html` and `work.html`; both updated. The "Capability Session" description recurs in `work.html` and `diagnostic.html`; left consistent (entry rung).

**Routing/links:** homepage card now points to `work.html#individual-path` (anchor added). New `/executive-portfolio` rewrite added.

**Forms affected:** new interest form on the pilot page posts to the existing Formspree endpoint with `source: 'executive-portfolio'` and a Plausible event. No change to existing forms.

**Responsive risks:** the new pilot page uses the established breakpoints; the deliverables grid collapses to one column < 900px. Fixed an unrelated pre-existing overflow in the Work "Ready to start" grid.

**Accessibility:** single `<h1>` per page, sequential headings, visually-hidden `<label>`s on every form field, focus states inherited from the design system, `prefers-reduced-motion` respected via `styles.css`.

**Assumptions / provisional decisions (hold until the pilot validates):**
- **No public price** on the pilot page. Scope and investment are disclosed in conversation. Revisit once a founding client confirms the $5k–$12.5k band.
- Executive Portfolio stays `noindex` and off the nav/homepage until validated.
- "Founding cohort" framing is provisional; drop it once the offer is established.

## PHASE 8 — Deliverables

1. **Positioning ruling:** keep the architecture; fix offer hierarchy, naming, and CTAs. No redesign. Retire "coaching." Introduce the Executive Capability Portfolio as a discreet by-request pilot, not a homepage centerpiece.
2. **Offer hierarchy:** two pathways — Organizations (Diagnostic → Architecture Advisory → Speaking) and Individuals (Individual Capability Formation → Executive Capability Portfolio). Diagnostic is the shared front door.
3. **Language changed and why:** "Individual Capability Coaching" → "Individual Capability Formation" (retire the coaching read, uncap the price); new "Executive Capability Portfolio"; "waitlist" CTA → "Take the Diagnostic" / "Request a private conversation." Full rationale in Phase 3.
4. **Exact code changes:** see file-by-file summary below and the git diff on this branch.
5. **File-by-file summary:**
   - `index.html` — service card 04 relabelled to Individual Capability Formation; CTA now "See the individual path →" to `work.html#individual-path`.
   - `work.html` — offer 04 reframed (coaching retired, CTA fixed to the diagnostic); new discreet Executive Capability Portfolio pilot strip; `#individual-path` anchor added; "Ready to start" grid now collapses on mobile.
   - `executive-portfolio.html` *(new)* — noindex by-request pilot page with deliverables, audience, process, pilot note, and interest form.
   - `netlify.toml` — `/executive-portfolio` clean-URL rewrite.
   - `robots.txt` — disallow the pilot page.
6. **Testing results:** Phase 7 above — all green; one pre-existing, unrelated placeholder noted.
7. **Preview:** desktop and mobile screenshots captured for index, work, and the pilot page (shared in the session). Local preview: `python3 -m http.server` from the repo root.
8. **Unresolved issues:**
   - No attributed individual/executive proof yet — the biggest lever for the premium offer's credibility. Add a permissioned testimonial when available.
   - Pricing for the Portfolio is intentionally private until a founding client validates the $5k–$12.5k band.
   - Optional voice cleanups ("unlock" in About, "navigating" microcopy) left for a separate copy pass.
