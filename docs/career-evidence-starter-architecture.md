# Product architecture: Starter → Keep the Proof → Field Kit

One page. What each product is for, and why the free one does not cannibalise
the paid one.

---

## The three jobs

| | **Keep the Proof: The 10-Minute Career Evidence Starter** | **Keep the Proof** | **Capability Formation Field Kit** |
|---|---|---|---|
| Price | Free | $49 | $150 |
| The job | Prove **one** thing | Build the **record** | Read the **position** |
| The question it answers | What did I do, what changed, and how would I say it outside this company? | How do I capture, translate, organise and maintain all of it before I need it? | What is my current work building in me, and will it still matter when the context changes? |
| Time | About 10 minutes | About 60 minutes, then ongoing | One sitting, rerun quarterly |
| Output | One completed evidence entry and one portable Proof Line | A maintained career evidence record | A read of your capability position |
| Route | `/career-evidence-starter` | `/keep-the-proof` | `/fieldkit` |

The distinction in one line each:

- **The Starter** helps me prove one thing.
- **Keep the Proof** helps me build the record.
- **The Field Kit** helps me read the position.

---

## What the Starter includes

- One simplified Quick Capture: the work, roughly when, why it mattered
- A permission checklist, ticked before writing
- Six prompts: what was true before, my contribution, what changed, the
  evidence, what it shows I could do again, and the internal language to remove
- One Proof Line builder with a scaffold and one concise worked example
- One completed portable evidence statement as the final artifact
- A four-point self-check before the line gets used

Six pages. Fourteen fillable text fields and eight checkboxes.

## What Keep the Proof adds, and the Starter does not contain

- The reusable **Career Evidence Ledger**, twelve pages, used over time
- **Multiple** Quick Capture records rather than one
- The **Full Career Evidence Entry** system
- The **monthly Proof Sweep** and **quarterly Proof Review**
- The **Evidence Index**
- The **complete translation system**
- The full 60-minute implementation method
- The complete confidentiality teaching

## What the Field Kit adds, and neither of the others contains

Density and Optionality scoring, the Four States, boundary readings, the
Capability Position Read, and the rescore tracker. None of that appears in the
Starter. The Starter never diagnoses a state, never scores a dimension, and
never advises whether to stay or leave.

---

## Why this does not cannibalise Keep the Proof

**The Starter is complete for the promise it makes, and the promise is narrow.**
It promises one accomplishment turned into one portable line, and it delivers
exactly that. Nothing required to finish that job has been withheld to force an
upgrade. Someone who only ever wants one strong example for one interview is
genuinely served, and should not buy anything.

**The paid job is a different job, not more of the same one.** Keep the Proof is
not "the Starter, but longer". It is the difference between proving one thing
and maintaining a record: multiple entries, an index to find them, and monthly
and quarterly routines that keep the record current. A single sheet cannot do
that at any length, so there is no version of the free product that quietly
becomes the paid one.

**The desire it creates is the honest kind.** A user who finishes one entry
discovers two things at once: that the method works, and that they have exactly
one entry. The second discovery is the argument for the record, and it is made
by the result rather than by an artificial stopping point.

**The naming now carries the ladder.** Because the free product is branded
*Keep the Proof: The 10-Minute Career Evidence Starter*, the paid system is the
obvious next step in the same family rather than an unrelated upsell. The risk
that comes with that naming is confusion about what the free thing includes, and
the page and the PDF both answer it explicitly: the Starter's "what it is not"
list names the Ledger, and page 6 states plainly that this Starter proves one
thing while Keep the Proof builds the record.

**No product sits between them.** As instructed, nothing new was created in the
gap between $49 and $150.
