The Framework

Two readings. One resilience question.

The Four States show what the work is forming now. Alumni Capital shows whether value remains reachable after people leave. Together, they help leaders see whether capability can hold through change.

Capability Position

Density x Optionality

Density reads the depth of judgment the work is building. Optionality reads whether that capability can travel across roles, functions, industries, and cycles.

Four States Reading

Density and Optionality are read together to locate the current position across the Four States.

Separate organizational reading

Alumni Capital

Alumni Capital measures the value, relationships, and organizational reachability that remain after a person exits. It does not create a fifth state or a third placement axis.

What it reveals

Whether capability disappears at departure, remains accessible, or returns to the organization in new forms.
