Private Advisory · By Request

The Executive
Capability Portfolio.

A private advisory engagement for senior executives navigating a consequential transition. It is not coaching and it is not outplacement. It is a capability assessment and a written portfolio: a clear account of what you have built, what it is worth, where it travels, and how to carry it into the decision in front of you.

What It Is

Capability Formation, applied to one executive.

Senior careers are rarely lost for lack of capability. They stall because capability built in one context is hard to prove in the next, because a consequential decision arrives before the evidence is organized, or because the language a board uses is not the language the work was done in.

The Executive Capability Portfolio is the Capability Formation Framework worked through in depth for a single person. The organizational version reads a workforce. This reads one career: where it sits across Density and Optionality, what compounds, what is quietly narrowing, and what the next environment is actually worth.

What You Leave With
01
Capability assessment
A structured read of where your capability sits across Density and Optionality, what is compounding, and what is narrowing toward fragile specialization.
02
Capability translation
The work you have done, rendered in the terms a board, a search committee, or a new function will recognize, without inflating it.
03
Decision architecture
A clear structure for the specific decision in front of you: what the options actually cost, what each one builds or spends, and what you would be trading.
04
Portability analysis
An honest map of where your capability travels well, where it does not yet, and what closes the gap before the transition, not after.
05
Transition strategy
A sequenced approach to the move itself: what to build, what to leave well, and how to enter the next environment carrying your full weight.
06
A written portfolio
The engagement produces a substantive written document you keep. Not notes from a call. A capability portfolio you can think and decide with over the years that follow.
Who This Is For

Senior executives at a consequential point of transition.

The work assumes a career with real depth already built. It is most useful when a decision is close and the stakes are high enough that seeing the whole picture clearly is worth the time it takes.

→Executives weighing a move across function, sector, or company at senior level
→Leaders preparing for board consideration or a first board seat
→Operators leaving a long tenure and deciding what the next chapter should be built on
→Founders and executives after an exit, translating what they built into what comes next
→Senior leaders whose capability is real but has never been organized into evidence
How It Works

By request, and deliberately small.

01
A private conversation
You share what you are working through. We determine together whether this is the right instrument for it. If it is not, I will say so.
02

03
