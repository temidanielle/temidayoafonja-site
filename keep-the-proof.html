The problem

The hardest time to remember your work is when you suddenly need to explain it.

You know you contributed. You remember that the project mattered. But months or years later, the details are harder to recover.

What changed? What did you personally do? What evidence exists? How would you explain it to someone outside the company?

Keep the Proof helps you answer those questions while the work is still close enough to document accurately.

What changes

From a task description to an account of contribution.

Before

“Helped with onboarding.”

After

“Led the redesign of a cross-functional onboarding experience, coordinated stakeholders across the business, and improved one measure of new-hire integration from 47 to 75.”

The system moves you from a vague task description to a credible, evidence-supported account of what you contributed and what changed. Not every entry will carry a number. Where the evidence is qualitative, the system helps you say what changed and how you know.

Questions

Before you buy.

Are the PDFs fillable?

Yes. Both main documents contain fillable fields and are best used in Adobe Acrobat Reader.

Do I need to complete everything in one sitting?

No. The first 60 minutes help you establish the system and complete at least one meaningful entry. The ledger is designed for continued monthly and quarterly use.

Is this a resume template?

No. It helps you capture the underlying evidence that can later strengthen a resume, interview story, promotion case, portfolio, or career conversation.

Can I use this if I am not currently job searching?

Yes. The best time to preserve career evidence is before a transition becomes urgent.

Should I include confidential company information?

No. Use generalized, permitted descriptions and avoid confidential employee, customer, or proprietary business information.

How is this different from the Capability Formation Field Kit?

Keep the Proof helps you capture and translate evidence of work you have already done. The Field Kit helps you assess what your current work is building in you and read your broader capability position.
