The problem

You know you did meaningful work. But months later, can you still explain exactly what changed, what part was yours, and why another employer should care?

The details fade first. Then the numbers become hard to recover, your contribution blurs into the team's, and the language you used at the time stops making sense to anyone outside the company.

The Starter takes one accomplishment and turns it into an evidence statement while the work is still close enough to document accurately.

What you leave with

Three things, in one focused sitting.

  • One completed Career Evidence Entry, built from a real piece of your own work
  • One portable Proof Line you can adapt for a review, an interview or a conversation
  • One way to repeat the thinking on the next piece of work worth keeping

Set aside about 10 to 15 focused minutes.

How it works

Six prompts, one line at the end.

1

Choose one accomplishment

A project, decision, problem, improvement or piece of work where something changed because you were involved.

2

Build the evidence

What was true before, what you specifically contributed, what changed, and what evidence supports the account.

3

Write the Proof Line

Strip out the internal language and put it in one sentence someone outside your company would understand.

Inside your company

Project Horizon
QBR
XYZ Workflow

→
Outside your company

Cross-functional onboarding redesign
Operating review
Customer intake process

This is an example of the distinction, not a claim that those terms are equivalent in every workplace.

What it is

  • A free six-page fillable PDF
  • One quick capture, six prompts and one Proof Line builder
  • A permission checklist so nothing you are not allowed to keep goes into it
  • Yours to save, reuse and print

What it is not

  • Not a resume-writing service
  • Not a job-search course
